# Japan 2026 — Alternate Skeleton (Cut Kanazawa, Add Matsumoto)

Reference-only day skeleton. Variant of [2026-fall-itinerary.md](./2026-fall-itinerary.md) that drops Kanazawa (route/logistics felt too packed with 4 stops) and replaces it with a Takayama → Matsumoto → Tokyo return leg (Option B: overnight in Matsumoto rather than a same-day transit slog). Freed-up days went to Nagoya (+1) and Takayama (+1), per the group's preference to keep Nagoya rather than cut it.

Not yet adopted — this is for comparing against the original skeleton before deciding.

## Day skeleton (16 days)

| Days | Dates | Location | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1–4 | Tue Oct 27 – Fri Oct 30 | Tokyo | Unchanged from original skeleton |
| 5 | Sat Oct 31 | Tokyo → Nagoya | Tokaido Shinkansen (~1h40). Evening in Nagoya |
| 6–7 | Sun Nov 1 – Mon Nov 2 | Nagoya | **+1 day vs. original.** Two full days — room for Nagoya Castle, Osu, city views (Mirai Tower/Oasis 21), food/drink list, without rushing |

| 14–16 | Mon Nov 9 – Wed Nov 11 | Tokyo | Unchanged from original skeleton. Nov 11 is departure day (flight 5:15 PM) |

## What changed vs. the original skeleton

- **Cut:** Kanazawa entirely (Kenrokuen, Higashi Chaya, 21st Century Museum, Omicho Market, snow crab, Korinbo illumination — all off the table in this version)
- **Added:** Matsumoto — Matsumoto Castle + old town, roughly a half-day of sightseeing since most of the day is transit
- **Nagoya:** 1.5 days → 2.5 days (arrival evening + 2 full days)
- **Takayama:** ~1.5 days + Shirakawa-go day trip → ~2.5 days + Shirakawa-go day trip
- **Route shape:** No longer a clean loop — Kanazawa's direct Hokuriku Shinkansen back to Tokyo is replaced by a bus + limited express combo through Nagano prefecture. Total inter-city transit time is similar, but it's a bus leg instead of a shinkansen leg for that stretch
