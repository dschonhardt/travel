# Full reviews

## Overall trip review

**Verdict:** A strong, well-researched plan. The route makes sense geographically (Tokyo → Nagoya → Takayama → Kanazawa → Tokyo, no backtracking), the foliage timing for Kamikochi on Nov 3 and Kanazawa Nov 4–7 is right, and the crab-opening lunch on Nov 7 is a great catch. The weak spots are not the content. They are (a) a pile of bookings that still aren't made with 3 days to go, (b) luggage on the Hida/Toyama legs, (c) eleven straight early-start days (Oct 28–Nov 7) with no true rest day, and (d) a holiday-weekend Kamikochi plan that's a bit too relaxed about crowds. **Fun rating: 8.5/10** (9+ with the fixes below).
