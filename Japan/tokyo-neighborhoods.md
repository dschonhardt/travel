## West side

### Shimokitazawa

Odakyu Line from Shinjuku (~8 min), or the Keio Inokashira Line from Shibuya (~5 min). From Ryogoku, ~40 min via Shinjuku. Tokyo's indie neighborhood: tight lanes of thrift stores, record shops, curry houses and small live venues. Allow 4–5 hrs.

**On our dates:** nothing found. The curry festival ends Oct 25, and Bonus Track's site is down for maintenance, so its events couldn't be checked. Live house SHELTER has small J-rock shows on Nov 7 and 8 ([eplus](https://eplus.jp/sf/live/kanto/p69)).

- ☐ **Shimokita Senrogai Open Space** 🟢 — open lot on the old rail line with rotating food trucks and the Akichi Cafe & Bar (cafe 11:00–23:00, irregular closing days). **No November trucks posted yet** (only vantacos in late October). Check Instagram @senrogai_akichi the week before ([official](https://senrogai.com/akichi/))
- ☐ **Bonus Track** — low-rise strip of ~9 indie shops around a plaza: the B&B bookstore, the Tsukihi diary shop, Hakko Department (fermented foods)
- ☐ **reload** — white terraced complex on the old track toward Higashi-Kitazawa, with Ogawa Coffee Laboratory. Go up to the top terraces
- ☐ **Mikan Shimokita** — food and shops under the Inokashira Line overpass by the East Exit
- ☐ **Thrift and vintage crawl** — the backstreets north of the station (Ichibangai) and south. Cheaper than Harajuku
- ☐ **Ushitora** — ~35 Japanese craft taps. Ushitora Two is a standing bar with kushiage and oden. Possibly closed Mondays. Old source (2014), so confirm it's still going ([Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/tokyo/bars-and-pubs/ushitora))
- ☐ **Tateba Tengoku** — standing izakaya with ¥200 small plates and Hoppy ([Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/tokyo/shopping/tateba-tengoku), listing)
- ☐ **Curry** — Shimokita is a curry town. Pick a soup-curry or spice-curry shop for lunch (not checked)

### Ebisu

JR Yamanote one stop from Shibuya. From Ryogoku, ~35–40 min on the Hibiya Line from Akihabara. More grown-up than Shibuya, and good for a beer-and-izakaya evening.

**On our dates:** 📅 **YEBISU Marché**, Sun Nov 8, 11:00–17:00 at Château Plaza ([official](https://gardenplace.jp/event/)). Weekday food trucks at Clock Plaza (Mon–Fri 11:30–14:30). The Christmas illumination starts Nov 14, after we leave.

- ☐ **Yebisu Brewery Tokyo** — museum and taproom at Yebisu Garden Place. Weekends 11:00–19:00 (last entry 18:30), weekdays 12:00–20:00, **closed Tuesdays**, cashless only, entry free. The "YEBISU the JOURNEY" tour (~45 min, 20 people, in Japanese) **must be booked online**. Altered hours Nov 18–21 don't affect us ([official](https://www.sapporobeer.jp/brewery/y_museum/))
- ☐ **Ebisu Yokocho** — ~20 izakaya stalls in one covered building. Most stalls open ~17:00 until 3–5 AM. Not confirmed open every day ([Japan Journeys](https://japanjourneys.jp/tokyo/ebisu/dining/ebisu-yokocho/))
- ☐ **AFURI Ebisu** — the original shop of the yuzu-shio ramen chain. Daily 11:00–5:00 AM, until the soup runs out. **If you liked the yuzu ramen in Kanda** ([listing](https://www.gltjp.com/ja/directory/item/17221/))
- ☐ **buri** — classic standing bar 3 min from the station, open late ([guide](https://www.correc.co.jp/matchlife/encounter/371608))
- ☐ **Ebisu Dagashi Bar** — izakaya with free all-you-can-eat Showa-era candy ([Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/tokyo/bars-and-pubs/ebisu-dagashi-bar))
- ☐ **Tokyo Photographic Art Museum** — Garden Place, 10:00–18:00, closed Mondays
- ☐ **Osteria Bar Bacara** — new Venetian-style standing bar in the renovated Atre Ebisu (opened fall 2026, not checked)

### Nakameguro + Daikanyama

Hibiya Line or Tokyu Toyoko Line, one stop from Ebisu or two from Shibuya. Easy to add to an Ebisu day.

**On our dates:** 📅 **Craft Beer Japan Hop Fest** at Spring Valley Brewery (Log Road Daikanyama, 7 min walk from Ebisu), Sat Nov 7 and Sun Nov 8. See the calendar above. The fresh-hop IBUKI beer comes out Nov 7.

- ☐ **Hop Fest / Spring Valley Brewery Tokyo** 📅 — on normal days, Mon–Sat 11–23, Sun 11–22
- ☐ **Baird Beer Nakameguro Taproom** — GT Plaza C 2F. **Open daily**, weekdays 17:00–23:00, Sun 12:00–22:00 ([official](https://bairdbeer.com/pages/nakameguro-taproom))
- ☐ **Meguro River walk** — tree-lined canal with cafes and shops along both banks. Famous for cherry blossoms, quieter in November
- ☐ **Starbucks Reserve Roastery Tokyo** — four-floor Kengo Kuma building on the river, with a roastery, a tea bar and a cocktail bar
- ☐ **Nakameguro Koukashita** — shops and bars in the arches under the train tracks
- ☐ **Meguro Ginza shopping street** — grocers, vintage shops and bakeries ~2 min south-west of the station
- ☐ **Daikanyama T-Site** — design-focused bookstore complex, ~10 min walk uphill
- ~~Beer Boy Nakameguro~~ — **marked closed** on Beer Tengoku since Nov 2024 ([source](https://beertengoku.com/?p=20153)). Use Baird instead

### Sangenjaya

Tokyu Den-en-toshi Line, 2 stops from Shibuya. Mostly residential, with a dense bar scene by the station.

**On our dates:** 📅 **Sangenjaya Art Music Festival, Sat Nov 7.** Free daytime stages 11:00–18:00. From ~20:00 the **Sancha Night Loop** runs until 4–5 AM through five bars, including in Sankaku Chitai (BORRACHOS, Umechan bar 32016, FRIENDS, Niwar's, DJ BAR Tengu Shokudo). **¥3,000 cash wristband** = one Sancha-Hi per bar, 20+. The ticketed HEAVEN'S DOOR show isn't needed ([official](https://sangenjaya-mf.com/)). **The Golden Gai-style bar crawl, with a festival on top, on our arrival night.** Daikanyama's Hop Fest is ~15 min away via Shibuya.

- ☐ **Sankaku Chitai** — postwar black-market triangle of tiny bars and yakitori. Younger local crowd, few tourists. **If you liked Golden Gai**
- ☐ **Izakaya Hoshigumi** (Sankaku Chitai) — shio-nikomi (salt-broth offal stew) ~¥500 ([aumo](https://aumo.jp/articles/20454), listing)
- ☐ **Senta Honten** — big yakitori skewers ~¥160 (same source)
- ☐ **Yakushu Bar** — 6-seat herbal-spirits counter behind a small door with a green light
- ☐ **Carrot Tower** — free observation floor, 9:30–23:00, closed 2nd Wednesdays (Nov 11) ([rurubu](https://rurubu.jp/andmore/spot/80011748))

### Koenji

JR Chuo Line, a few minutes west of Shinjuku. The Chuo-Sobu local runs from Ryogoku without a change (~40 min). Vintage, punk and cheap drinking, and less polished than Shimokitazawa.

**On our dates:**
- 📅 **35th Ashiba Matsuri, Sun Nov 8, 11:00–18:30** (hours marked provisional), free. Okinawan food and Orion beer on the North Exit plaza, plus an Eisa drum parade along the Pal arcade. Goes ahead in light rain ([official](https://ashiba.seinen-bu.net/) · [listing](https://www.enjoytokyo.jp/event/2081824/))
- 📅 **Punk Rock Connection 2026, Sat Nov 7** — six live houses (HIGH, SHOWBOAT, CLUB ROOTS, Kiraku, STUDIO BAYD, KATA_BAR). Times and prices not found ([listing](https://www.ragnet.co.jp/music-festival-autumn-east/18))
- **Moving Koenji to Sunday** would catch the festival, and Kosugi-yu opens at 8 AM on weekends

- ☐ **North-side shopping streets** — vintage and thrift, some real embroidered sukajan jackets if you dig
- ☐ **South-side Pal and Look arcades** — covered streets with more thrift, plus cheap food (not checked)
- ☐ **Kosugi-yu** — neighborhood sento, tattoo friendly. Weekdays 14:00–25:30, **Sat/Sun 8:00–25:30**, closed Thursdays ([official](https://kosugiyu.co.jp/))
- ☐ **Izakaya under the tracks** — bars and izakaya in the arches between Koenji and Asagaya (not checked)
- ☐ **Kitakore Building** — small, wild building of avant-garde secondhand fashion (not checked)
- ☐ **Koenji Hikawa Shrine** — has Japan's only shrine to the weather. Quick stop (not checked)
- ☐ **Nogata Hope** 🟢 — ~15 min walk north, see [Nogata](#nogata)

### Nogata

Seibu Shinjuku Line, or ~15 min walk north from JR Koenji. Plain local shotengai with cheap old-school eats and almost no tourists.

**On our dates:** nothing found.

- ☐ **Nogata Hope** 🟢 — triple-soup tonkotsu ramen, classic 元 (Gen) ¥950 ([official menu](https://www.nogata-hope.com/menu), Oct 25). Main shop 2-58-4 Nogata, **11:00–3:00 AM, no closing day**. **Cash, or PayPay only** (since July 2026) ([official](https://www.nogata-hope.com/store)). Branches in **Nakano** (to 1:30 AM) and **Kichijoji** (to 2:00 AM) if Nogata doesn't fit. The Ogikubo branch is closed for building work until ~Jan 2027
- ☐ **Shotengai walk** — Showa-era shopping streets and cheap izakaya along the Seibu line (not checked)

### Nakano

JR Chuo Line, one stop past Koenji. The Chuo-Sobu local goes direct from Ryogoku (~35 min).

**On our dates:** nothing found. The Nakano Nigiwai Festa was Nov 9–10 in 2024, but no fall 2026 edition turned up, so check the week before.

- ☐ **Nakano Broadway** — retro 1960s mall. **Mandarake: 12:00–20:00, open every day** ([official](https://www.mandarake.co.jp/dir/nkn/)), with ~25–35 shops inside: manga, figures, retro games and trading cards. Ignore nbw.jp, which is no longer the mall's site
- ☐ **Yakitori no Meimon Akiyoshi, Nakano** 🟢 — 5-54-3 Nakano, Fujita Bldg 1F, 03-3385-0071. **Open daily**, weekdays 17:00–22:30, weekends 16:00–22:00. **The reliable version of Adam's pick** ([official](https://www.akiyoshi.co.jp/shop/tokyo/index.html))
- ☐ **Nogata Hope Nakano** 🟢 — 11:00–1:30 AM
- ☐ **Nakano Sun Mall** — the covered arcade from the station to Broadway
- ☐ **Back-alley bars** — the lanes east of Sun Mall are packed with tiny izakaya and bars (not checked)

### Kichijoji

JR Chuo Line, past Koenji (~45 min from Ryogoku). One of Tokyo's favorite neighborhoods to live in, with a big park and a drinking alley.

**On our dates:** nothing found. The Parco flea market is Nov 1 and 14. The Inokashira Park Art Market (weekends) may run Sun Nov 8, but its 2026–27 season isn't confirmed.

- ☐ **Inokashira Park** — pond with rowboats, and autumn color around mid-November
- ☐ **Harmonica Yokocho** — tight grid of tiny bars and stalls by the station. **If you liked Omoide Yokocho**
- ☐ **Satou** — menchi-katsu (fried minced-meat cutlet), closed only at New Year. Queues, so go before lunch. The steakhouse upstairs is open 11:00–14:30 and 17:00–20:00 ([official](https://www.shop-satou.com/kichijoji.html))
- ☐ **Beer Boy Kichijoji** 🟢 — the original branch, a seated izakaya with ~10 rotating craft taps and yakitori. 2-8-8 Kichijoji Minamicho. Mon–Fri 17:00–1:00 AM, weekends from 15:00. 2020 source ([Japan Journeys](https://japanjourneys.jp/lifestyle/dining/bars/beer-boy-kichijoji-shibuya-nakameguro/))
- ☐ **Nogata Hope Kichijoji** 🟢 — basement shop, 11:00–2:00 AM
- ☐ **Iseya** — old yakitori by the park. The park branch is listed as closed Mondays, 12:00–22:00 ([Gurunavi](https://r.gnavi.co.jp/d3mfav0k0000/))
- ☐ **Sun Road arcade** — covered shopping street from the station
- ~~Ghibli Museum~~ — **closed Nov 4–18, 2026**, and every Tuesday ([official](https://www.ghibli-museum.jp/en/tickets/))

### Ogikubo

JR Chuo Line, one stop past Koenji/Asagaya. Known as one of Tokyo's ramen towns.

**On our dates:** nothing found. The local festival is Nov 15.

- ☐ **Ogikubo-style ramen** — old-school soy-sauce chuka soba shops around the station, such as **Harukiya** (not checked)
- ☐ **Otaguro Park** — small garden with a ginkgo avenue that turns gold in late November (not checked)
- ⚠️ **Akiyoshi Ogikubo** 🟢 — **not on the chain's official Tokyo store list** ([official](https://www.akiyoshi.co.jp/shop/tokyo/index.html)), so it has probably closed. Use **Akiyoshi Nakano** instead. **Nogata Hope Ogikubo** is also closed until ~Jan 2027

### Togoshi Ginza

Tokyu Ikegami Line to Togoshi-Ginza, or Toei Asakusa Line to Togoshi (~45 min from Ryogoku). One of Tokyo's longest shopping streets (~1.3 km), very local.

**On our dates:** nothing found.

- ☐ **Togoshi Ginza Onsen** — sento with natural black-water hot spring, ¥550 (¥950 with sauna). Usually 15:00–1:00 AM, **plus a Sunday-morning bath 8:00–12:00**. Closed Fridays ([listing](https://www.gltjp.com/ja/directory/item/12758/)). **The strongest reason to come here**
- ☐ **The shopping street** — walk end to end, snacking on croquettes and fried food
- ☐ **Onigiri Togoshiya** — 40+ kinds of onigiri from ¥280. Weekends 10:00–21:00 ([Time Out, 2024](https://www.timeout.jp/tokyo/ja/%E3%83%AC%E3%82%B9%E3%83%88%E3%83%A9%E3%83%B3/togoshiya))
- ☐ **Kinkaro** — local Chinese restaurant known for its shrimp fried rice ([Tokyu](https://www.tokyu.co.jp/area/togoshi-ginza/))
- ☐ **Togoshi Hachiman Shrine** — quiet, leafy shrine just off the street

### Takadanobaba

### Jimbocho

Near Kanda, ~15 min. Used-bookstore district and Tokyo's curry capital. **If you liked the Kanda ramen spot.** **Avoid Sunday**: Lunchon, Sabouru and Kitchen Nankai are all closed.

**On our dates:** the book festival and curry Grand Prix end Nov 1. The Kanda curry stamp rally runs to Dec 20 (minor).

- ☐ **Beer Hall Lunchon** — beer hall since 1909, with draft beer, beef pie and menchi-katsu. Mon–Fri 11:30–21:30, Sat to 20:30, **closed Sundays** ([Chiyoda tourism](https://visit-chiyoda.tokyo/app/spot/detail/373))
- ☐ **Bondy** — European-style curry. **11:00–22:30, open all our dates** ([Chiyoda tourism](https://visit-chiyoda.tokyo/app/spot/detail/376))
- ☐ **Kitchen Nankai** — katsu curry. 11:15–15:00 and 17:00–19:30, **closed Sundays** ([Time Out](https://www.timeout.jp/tokyo/ja/レストラン/kitchen-nankai-jinbocho))
- ☐ **Sabouru** — classic Showa kissaten, Mon–Sat 9:30–23:00, **closed Sundays**. A Wed Nov 11 morning coffee works ([Chiyoda tourism](https://visit-chiyoda.tokyo/app/spot/detail/482))
- ☐ **Tachinomi Uokatsu** — standing bar with fresh fish and sake (not checked)
- ☐ **Used bookstores** — hundreds, some with English-language and art sections. **Shosen Grande** for hobby and railway books

### Shibamata

Keisei Line, ~35–40 min. Good half day. **If you liked Asakusa and retro arcades.** **Sunday is the day**, since the toy museum is weekends only and the eel shop is closed Tuesdays.

**On our dates:** no temple fair. The Koshin fair days come every 60 days (Oct 13 and Dec 12 this year).

- ☐ **Taishakuten temple** — the carved-wood gallery and Suikei-en garden, ¥400, ~9:00–16:00 ([rurubu](https://rurubu.jp/andmore/spot/80012271))
- ☐ **Shopping street to the temple** — kusa-dango at **Takagiya Roho** (9:00–17:00), eel at **Kawachiya** (**closed Tuesdays**)
- ☐ **Shibamata Haikara Yokocho** — retro pinball, old candy and games, ~10:00–18:00 with irregular closures. **Toy museum upstairs is open Sat/Sun only** ([official](https://www.haikarayokotyo.com/))
- ☐ **Tora-san Museum** — 9:00–17:00, closed 3rd Tuesday (Nov 17), so open on our dates
- ☐ **Yagiri no Watashi** — tiny hand-rowed ferry across the Edo River. **Runs daily through November**, ~10:00–16:00, ¥300 ([Maruchiba](https://maruchiba.jp/spot/detail_10042.html))

### Funabashi + Makuhari (Chiba)

Over the Chiba line on Tokyo Bay. Sobu local from Ryogoku straight to **Tsudanuma** (~35–40 min), then the beer garden's free shuttle bus. **For Adam's "Sapporo Beer Factory"** ([his map pin](https://maps.app.goo.gl/jtcx5PQhByGmaA1D7)). An evening trip: dinner is the reason to go.

**On our dates:** nothing found. Funabashi racecourse's night racing is Nov 2–6, before we're back in Tokyo ([official](https://www.f-keiba.com/)).

- ☐ **Sapporo Beer Chiba Brewery** 🟢 — 2 Takasecho, Funabashi. **No factory tours**: Sapporo lists Chiba under "other factories (no factory tours)" ([official](https://www.sapporobeer.jp/brewery/))
- ☐ **Sapporo Beer Chiba Beer Garden** 🟢 — the brewery's beer restaurant on the grounds, run by Ginza Lion, with a Tokyo Bay view. Mon–Thu 12:00–21:00, Fri 12:00–22:00, Sat 11:00–22:00, Sun 11:00–21:00 (Google Maps). Jingisukan Lamb Set (6 cuts), wagyu yakiniku and seafood hamayaki sets, 100-min all-you-can-drink with Black Label, and an all-you-can-eat jingisukan option. **Book online** (phones closed), 2-hr seating, 100% fee for same-day cancels. Free first-come shuttle (~50 seats) from JR Shin-Narashino (~10 min), Keisei Tsudanuma (~20 min) or JR Tsudanuma (~25 min); timetable not checked ([Tabelog](https://tabelog.com/en/chiba/A1202/A120201/12000122/))
- ☐ **Funabashi Sanbanze Seaside Park** — tidal flats and a sandy beach for sunset over the bay with Tokyo's lights, and Mt. Fuji on clear days. 9:00–17:00 (Google Maps), free. Sunset is ~4:40 PM. Diamond Fuji is late October, so we miss it. Awkward to reach: Keisei bus from Keisei Funabashi to "Funabashi Kaihin Koen" (ride time not checked), with no easy link on to the beer garden. The beer garden has its own bay view, so skip this unless someone really wants the beach ([Funabashi City](https://www.city.funabashi.lg.jp/funabashistyle/en/003/p042829.html))
- ☐ **Costco Makuhari** — 1-4 Toyosuna, Mihama-ku. 10:00–20:00 daily (Google Maps). Near JR Makuhari-Toyosuna on the Keiyo Line, one stop from Shin-Narashino (walk not checked). US membership card works. Japan-only snacks, sake and food court items
- ☐ **AEON Mall Makuhari New City** — next to Costco at 1-1 Toyosuna. 10:00–21:00 daily (Google Maps). One of Japan's biggest malls (~350 shops, renovated 2023–24), split into Grand, Ekimae, Active and Pet malls. Mostly a big suburban mall, so it's a filler while others do Costco, not a reason to go. The more unusual bits: a **namco arcade**, the **Yoshimoto comedy theater** (Japanese-language manzai), the **Active Mall** (outdoor and sports brands, a climbing wall and a 3-on-3 court) and the **Pet Mall** (people bring dogs in strollers). Directly connected to Makuhari-Toyosuna station ([GLT Japan](https://www.gltjp.com/en/directory/item/11847/) · [att-japan](https://att-japan.net/en/aeon-makuhari_asm/))
- ☐ **Nakayama Hokekyo-ji** — head temple of Nichiren Buddhism's Nakayama branch, 2-10-1 Nakayama, Ichikawa. 9:00–16:00 (Google Maps), free. Its Important Cultural Properties include the five-story pagoda, the soshi-do hall with its rare double-gabled roof, the Hokke-do and the four-legged gate. A bronze seated Buddha (Nakayama Daibutsu) sits on the grounds. Stone-paved approach from Keisei Nakayama (~5 min), or ~10 min from JR Shimousa-Nakayama on the Sobu local line (walk not checked). The antique market only runs during the April and November festivals, and the November date wasn't found ([tabi-mag](https://tabi-mag.jp/hokekyouji))
- ☐ **Funabashi Beer Brewery (Nishi-Funabashi)** — brewpub 1 min from Nishi-Funabashi station, the transfer on the way home. Tue–Fri 16:00–23:00, Sat–Sun 14:00–23:00, **closed Mondays** (Google Maps). House Funabashi Ale, plus Funabashi Black, a stout made with honbinos clams. Glasses from ¥550 ([Gurunavi](https://r.gnavi.co.jp/kaa9mjuv0000/))
- ☐ **Honbinos clams** — Funabashi's local shellfish, a big hard clam from the Sanbanze flats (Chiba-brand seafood since 2017). **Sanbanze Minatoya**, the fishermen's co-op shop at Funabashi port (1-22-1 Hinode): 10:30–15:00, **closed Tue and Wed** (Google Maps). To eat them: **Ikkyu Funabashi** (izakaya 1 min from Funabashi station, clam fry and sake-steamed clams) or **Funabashi-ya** in Nishi-Funabashi (clam kushiage). Hours for both not checked ([Funabashi City](https://www.city.funabashi.lg.jp/funabashistyle/jp/005/p042569.html) · [Maruchiba](https://maruchiba.jp/gourmet/spot/detail_11072.html))
- ☐ **Funabashi Daijingu (Ohi Shrine)** — the city's main shrine, by Keisei Funabashi Ohjingu-shita, open 24 hours (Google Maps). An old wooden lighthouse stands on the grounds (not checked)
- ☐ **LaLaport Tokyo-Bay + IKEA Tokyo-Bay** — giant mall and IKEA at Minami-Funabashi, if anyone wants to shop (not checked)
- **Not worth it this trip:** Chiba City (Port Tower, the suspended monorail) is another 20–30 min past Makuhari for not much. Makuhari Messe has nothing on Nov 9–10, and its trade shows start Nov 11 ([tradefairdates](https://www.tradefairdates.com/Makuhari+Messe-ZS852.html))

**A day out here (best Tue Nov 10 or Sun Nov 8; on Mon Nov 9 the brewpub is closed).** Times are rough, and the train times are estimates, not checked.

| Est. time | What |
|---|---|
| 11:30 AM | Sobu local from Ryogoku to **Shimousa-Nakayama** (~25 min) |
| 12:00 PM | Walk to **Hokekyo-ji**: approach, pagoda, soshi-do, daibutsu (~1 hr). Lunch on the way, or just a snack to save room |
| 1:30 PM | Sobu local to Nishi-Funabashi, then the Musashino/Keiyo Line to **Makuhari-Toyosuna** (~30 min) |
| 2:15 PM | **Costco** and/or **AEON Mall** (~2 hrs). Food court snack if hungry |
| 4:15 PM | Keiyo Line one stop to **Shin-Narashino**, then the shuttle (~10 min) to the beer garden |
| 4:30 PM | **Chiba Beer Garden**: sunset over the bay (~4:40), then jingisukan and Black Label. 2-hr seating, so book ~4:30 online |
| 6:45 PM | Shuttle back to Shin-Narashino, then the Keiyo/Musashino Line to **Nishi-Funabashi** for a nightcap at **Funabashi Beer Brewery** (open to 11 PM, not Mondays) |
| ~8:45 PM | Sobu local back to Ryogoku (~25 min) |

**Shorter version (evening only):** leave Ryogoku ~3:00 PM, Costco, then the beer garden at sunset and home. ~4 hrs total.
