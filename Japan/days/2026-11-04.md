# Day 9 — Wed Nov 4 — Takayama → Kanazawa (morning market, train via Toyama)

## Plan
All times are rough estimates.

| Est. time | What |
|---|---|
| 7:45 AM | Check out early (the hotel is across from the station) and leave bags at the front desk |
| 8:00 AM | Walk ~10 min to the **Takayama morning markets**, which open at **7:00 AM Nov–Mar** (6:00 the rest of the year) and close ~noon. Start at **Jinya-mae Asaichi** (in front of Takayama Jinya), then cross the river to **Miyagawa Asaichi**. Breakfast is grazing at the stalls: pickled veg, tamagoyaki, mitarashi dango, Hida beef skewers. Before 9 AM is still ahead of most tour buses |
| 9:30 AM | Optional: coffee in the old town, or one last look at Sanmachi Suji (a few minutes from Miyagawa) |
| 10:00 AM | Optional: **Hida Takayama Retro Museum (Showa-kan)**, opens 10:00 AM, ¥1,000. Retro 1950s townscape, playable arcade games, candy shop. Give it 30–40 min only, and leave by 10:40 |
| 10:45 AM | Back to the hotel for bags, then across to the station (~10 min walk from the old town) |
| **11:03 AM** | **Hida 3** departs Takayama for Toyama. It's the only morning Hida to Toyama; the next is Hida 7 at 1:17 PM (Toyama 2:51) |
| 12:32 PM | Arrive **Toyama**. Transfer to Shinkansen or Ainokaze Toyama Railway for Kanazawa |
| 1:15 PM | Arrive **Kanazawa**. Drop bags at the hotel (check-in is later) |
| 1:30 PM | Late lunch at the station, so Omicho stays fresh for Fri and Sat: **Maimon Sushi** (conveyor belt, inside Kanazawa Station; good nodoguro and local shrimp) or ramen in the station's Hyakubangai. Then check in and rest. It's been a 7:45 AM start |
| *Optional, 2:00 PM* | **Taproom BREW CLASSIC** (1-3-3 Katamachi), the award-winning local brewery's taproom, 6 taps. **Only open Wed–Fri 11 AM–3 PM** on our dates, so today is the one easy shot. Bus ~15 min from the station; last call before 3. Then back to the hotel to check in and rest |
| 4:30 PM | Easy walk (~20 min, or bus) to **Korinbo**. Browse the **Tatemachi** shopping street |
| 5:00 PM | **Korinbo Tree Fantasy** illumination comes on (free, 5 PM–midnight) |
| 5:15 PM | A pint at **Korinbo Jibiruba**, Kanazawa's first craft beer bar (2013): wood walls covered in beer stickers, kegs for furniture, rotating taps. Saving Futa's for the group tomorrow night |
| 6:30 PM | **Dinner in Katamachi**: **Ichinokura**, which has crab from its tank all year, walk-ins OK, counter seats (good solo). Get the crab-miso shell roast and some **Kanazawa oden** |
| ~8:30 PM | Back to the hotel (bus or ~25 min walk) |

**Kept light on purpose.** Everything big is saved for when Jeff and Adam are here: Kenrokuen, the castle, Omicho, Nagamachi and Nomura House, and Myoryuji on [Nov 6](./2026-11-06.md); Higashi Chaya and the Omicho crab lunch on [Nov 7](./2026-11-07.md).

**If Myoryuji still isn't booked:** go in person this afternoon and book at the intercom by the temple entrance (it stops taking visitors at 4:00 PM in winter). It's ~15 min on foot from Korinbo, so leave the hotel by ~3:15 and do Korinbo after.

## Travel
- Check out: **Hida Takayama Washington Hotel Plaza** — before 10:00 AM
- **Hida Express** Takayama → Toyama (~1.5 hrs), then **Shinkansen or Ainokaze Toyama Railway** Toyama → Kanazawa (~18–40 min). ~2 hrs total, more flexible timing than the bus

## Lodging
- Check in: **Hotel Livemax Kanazawaekimae** (booked) — Night 1 of 3
- Jeff and Adam arrive tomorrow (Nov 5), so the room needs to sleep 3 from then on

**Line status:** the Takayama Line's Sugihara–Inotani section (between Takayama and Toyama) was closed Mar 18–May 30, 2026, after bridge damage, and closed again for a day of heavy rain on Sep 9. It was running normally as of Oct 20. If it's closed on the day, take the **Nohi highway bus** Takayama → Kanazawa (~2¼ hrs) instead.
