# Day 9 — Wed Nov 4 — Takayama → Kanazawa (morning market, train via Toyama)

## Travel
- Check out: **Hida Takayama Washington Hotel Plaza** — before 10:00 AM
- **Hida Express** Takayama → Toyama (~1.5 hrs), then **Shinkansen or Ainokaze Toyama Railway** Toyama → Kanazawa (~18–40 min). ~2 hrs total, more flexible timing than the bus

## Lodging
- Check in: **Hotel Livemax Kanazawaekimae** (booked) — Night 1 of 3
- Jeff and Adam arrive tomorrow (Nov 5), so the room needs to sleep 3 from then on

## Plan
All times are rough estimates.

| Est. time | What |
|---|---|
| 6:30 AM | **Takayama morning market** — **Miyagawa Asaichi** (along the Miyagawa River) or **Jinya-mae Asaichi** (in front of Takayama Jinya). Both open ~6 AM, close ~noon. Go early before tour buses arrive and stalls get picked over |
| 7:30 AM | Breakfast at the market — grab pickled veg, tamagoyaki, snacks |
| 9:00 AM | Check out of hotel. Store bags if needed |
| 9:30 AM | **Hida Takayama Retro Museum (Showa-kan)** — opens 10:00 AM, ¥1,000. Walkable retro 1950s townscape, arcade games you can actually play, candy shop. ~1 hr. Skip if the morning ran long |
| 11:00 AM | **Hida Express departs** Takayama for Toyama |
| 12:30 PM | Arrive **Toyama**. Transfer to Shinkansen or Ainokaze Toyama Railway for Kanazawa |
| 1:15 PM | Arrive **Kanazawa**. Check in, drop bags |
| 2:30 PM | **Kenrokuen Garden** — one of Japan's three great gardens; good in the afternoon light. Yukitsuri (rope-bracing on the pine branches for snow) goes up ~Nov 1, so it'll already be in place |
| 4:30 PM | **Kanazawa Castle Park** — free to walk the grounds, right next to Kenrokuen |
| 5:30 PM | **Higashi Chaya District** — geisha teahouse quarter, gold-leaf shops, lantern-lit streets. Good first-night orientation |
| 7:00 PM | **Dinner** in Kanazawa — crab season opens tomorrow (Nov 6), so tonight is the last night before it's everywhere |
