# Day 9 — Wed Nov 4 — Takayama → Kanazawa (morning market, train via Toyama)

## Plan
All times are rough estimates.

| Est. time | What |
|---|---|
| 7:45 AM | Check out early (the hotel is across from the station) and leave bags at the front desk |
| 8:00 AM | Walk ~10 min to the **Takayama morning markets**, which close at noon. **Jinya-mae opens 6:00 AM**, **Miyagawa 7:00 AM**. Start at **Jinya-mae Asaichi** (in front of Takayama Jinya), then cross the river to **Miyagawa Asaichi**. Breakfast is grazing at the stalls: pickled veg, tamagoyaki, mitarashi dango, Hida beef skewers. Before 9 AM is still ahead of most tour buses |
| 9:30 AM | **Hida Takayama Retro Museum (Showa-kan)** (9:00 AM–5:00 PM daily, **¥1,200**): retro 1950s townscape, playable arcade games, candy shop. ~1 hr |
| **10:30 AM** | **Leave by 10:30.** Back to the hotel for bags (~12–15 min walk, ~1 km), then across to the station |
| **11:03 AM** | **Hida 3** departs Takayama for Toyama. It's the only morning Hida to Toyama; the next is Hida 7 at 1:17 PM (Toyama 2:51) |
| 12:32 PM | Arrive **Toyama**. Transfer to the Shinkansen: **Tsurugi 23 at 12:41** (9-min transfer, ¥2,860; buy the ticket ahead or ride unreserved). Missed it: **Hakutaka 559 at 1:14** (Kanazawa 1:36). Cheap option: Ainokaze/IR local **1:20 → 2:19** (¥1,290) |
| **1:03 PM** | Arrive **Kanazawa** (Tsurugi 23). Drop bags at the hotel (check-in is later) |
| 1:30 PM | Late lunch at the station, so Omicho stays fresh for Fri and Sat: **Maimon Sushi** (conveyor belt, inside Kanazawa Station, 11:00 AM–9:00 PM; good nodoguro and local shrimp) or ramen in the station's Hyakubangai |
| **~2:15 PM** | Bus (~15 min) or walk (~30 min) to **Myoryuji (Ninja Temple)**, 1-2-12 Nomachi, and **book 3 people for Fri Nov 6 at 2:30 PM** at the intercom on the left of the entrance (open 9:00 AM–4:00 PM). Backup slot: 3:00. Bring cash in case they want the ¥1,200 each up front. Skip this if the Takayama hotel already booked it (see TODO) |
| ~2:45 PM | Walk ~10 min back over the Sai River to **Taproom BREW CLASSIC** (1-3-3 Katamachi), the award-winning local brewery's taproom, 6 taps. Open **Wed–Sun 1:00–9:00 PM** (closed Mon–Tue) |
| ~4:00 PM | Walk ~10–15 min to **Korinbo**. Browse the **Tatemachi** shopping street. Check in at the hotel tonight; it's by the station |
| 5:00 PM | **Korinbo Tree Fantasy** illumination after dark (free) |
| 5:45 PM | **Dinner: Kanazawa curry at Turban Curry Honten** (ターバンカレー本店), Hirosaka 1-1-48, Unashin Bldg 1F, ~5 min walk from Korinbo. The original Turban shop (since 1971), one of the two founding Kanazawa-curry chains: thick black roux, katsu on top, shredded cabbage, eaten with a fork off a steel plate. Get the **loin katsu curry**. Open **11:00 AM–8:00 PM** daily |
| 6:45 PM | A pint at **Korinbo Jibiruba**, Kanazawa's first craft beer bar (2013): wood walls covered in beer stickers, kegs for furniture, rotating taps. **Opens 6:00 PM on weekdays** (3 PM weekends), closed Mondays, **cash only**. Saving Futa's for the group tomorrow night |
| 7:45 PM | Optional: walk ~10 min into **Katamachi** for a second, small stop at **Ichinokura** (5:00–11:00 PM; live crab tank all year, walk-ins OK, counter seats): the crab-miso shell roast (~¥1,700) and some **Kanazawa oden**. Or a nightcap in Katamachi |
| ~9:00 PM | Back to the hotel (bus or ~25 min walk) |

**Kept light on purpose.** Everything big is saved for when Jeff and Adam are here: Kenrokuen, the castle, Omicho, Nagamachi and Nomura House, and Myoryuji on [Nov 6](./2026-11-06.md); Higashi Chaya and the Omicho crab lunch on [Nov 7](./2026-11-07.md).

## Travel
- Check out: **Hida Takayama Washington Hotel Plaza** — before 10:00 AM
- **Hida Express** Takayama → Toyama (~1.5 hrs), then **Shinkansen** Toyama → Kanazawa (Tsurugi 23, 12:41 → 1:03, ~22 min) or the **Ainokaze/IR local** (1:20 → 2:19, ~1 hr). ~2 hrs total, more flexible timing than the bus

## Lodging
- Check in: **Hotel Livemax Kanazawaekimae** (booked) — Night 1 of 3
- Jeff and Adam arrive tomorrow (Nov 5), so the room needs to sleep 3 from then on (confirmed Oct 24)

**Line status:** the Takayama Line's Sugihara–Inotani section (between Takayama and Toyama) was closed Mar 18–May 30, 2026, after bridge damage, and closed again for a day of heavy rain on Sep 9. It was running normally as of Oct 20. If it's closed on the day, take the **Nohi highway bus** Takayama → Kanazawa (~2¼ hrs) instead.
