# Day 9 — Wed Nov 4 — Takayama → Kanazawa (morning market, train via Toyama)

## Plan
All times are rough estimates.

| Est. time | What |
|---|---|
| 6:30 AM | **Takayama morning market** — **Miyagawa Asaichi** (along the Miyagawa River) or **Jinya-mae Asaichi** (in front of Takayama Jinya). Both open ~6 AM, close ~noon. Go early before tour buses arrive and stalls get picked over |
| 7:30 AM | Breakfast at the market — grab pickled veg, tamagoyaki, snacks |
| 9:00 AM | Check out of hotel. Store bags if needed |
| 9:30 AM | **Hida Takayama Retro Museum (Showa-kan)** — opens 10:00 AM, ¥1,000. Walkable retro 1950s townscape, arcade games you can actually play, candy shop. ~1 hr. Skip if the morning ran long |
| 11:00 AM | **Hida Express departs** Takayama for Toyama |
| 12:30 PM | Arrive **Toyama**. Transfer to Shinkansen or Ainokaze Toyama Railway for Kanazawa |
| 1:15 PM | Arrive **Kanazawa**. Drop bags at the hotel (check-in is later) |
| 1:45 PM | **Omicho Market**: late lunch, a small kaisendon or snacks. Stalls start closing ~5 PM, so go now |
| 3:00 PM | **Oyama Shrine** (the stained-glass gate), then walk ~10 min to **Nagamachi Samurai District**: earthen walls and canals. Go inside **Nomura Samurai House** for its garden (check the closing time; last entry is likely ~4:00–4:30) |
| 5:00 PM | **Korinbo Tree Fantasy** illumination comes on (free, 5 PM–midnight), on the walk from Nagamachi to Katamachi |
| 6:30 PM | **Dinner in Katamachi**: **Ichinokura**, which has crab from its tank all year, walk-ins OK, counter seats (good solo). Get the crab-miso shell roast and some **Kanazawa oden** |
| ~8:30 PM | Back to the hotel (bus or ~25 min walk) |

**Saved for when Jeff and Adam are here:** Kenrokuen, the castle and Higashi Chaya. See [Nov 5](./2026-11-05.md) and [Nov 6](./2026-11-06.md).
