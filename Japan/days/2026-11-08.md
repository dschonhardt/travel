# Day 13 — Sun Nov 8 — Tokyo (Ryogoku and Kameido, Hop Fest in Daikanyama, Ebisu late)

**Draft (Oct 24).** Easy day near the hotel with no museums: a garden, a river walk, brunch at Edo Noren by the sumo ring, sumo and 47 Ronin history, then the chrysanthemum festival and gyoza in Kameido. A rest at the hotel, then across town in the evening to the **Craft Beer Japan Hop Fest** at Spring Valley Brewery in Daikanyama (18:00 session), and late bites in Ebisu next door. Replaces the Oct 23 west-side draft (see "Moved off today" below). Hours are from Google Maps unless marked.

## Plan
All times are rough estimates.

| Est. time | What |
|---|---|
| 9:30 AM | Slow start at the hotel |
| 10:00 AM | **Kyu-Yasuda Garden** (9:00–18:00, free), ~3 min from the hotel. Small Edo-era stroll garden around a pond that used to fill with the Sumida River's tides. ~30 min |
| 10:30 AM | **Sumida River walk** on the riverside terrace by Ryogoku Bridge. Loop back past the **Kokugikan** (sumo hall, outside only) and the sumo-town streets |
| 11:15 AM | **Brunch at Ryogoku Edo Noren** (10:00–23:00), the food hall inside the old 1929 Ryogoku station building, built around a full-size sumo ring. About a dozen Edo-style counters: sushi, tempura, soba, monja, chanko. Each counter keeps its own hours (not checked) |
| 12:30 PM | Walk ~10 min to **Ekoin Temple** (9:00–16:30): sumo tournaments were held here before the Kokugikan was built, and the grounds have sumo memorial stones. Then 3 min on to the **Kira residence site** (Honjo Matsuzakacho Park, always open), the small walled park where the 47 Ronin took their revenge in 1703 |
| 1:30 PM | JR Sobu Line 2 stops to Kameido. **Kameido Tenjin Shrine** (always open): the **chrysanthemum festival** (free, through Nov 23), the turtle pond and arched bridges with Skytree behind |
| 2:30 PM | **Kameido Gyoza**: one dish only, plates of small crispy gyoza, with a beer. The **Ryogoku branch** (4-34-10 Ryogoku; Sun 11:00–21:00) is the sure option, since the main shop's closing day is disputed |
| 3:15 PM | Back to the hotel to rest |
| 4:45 PM | Sobu Line to Akihabara, Hibiya Line to **Ebisu** (~40 min), walk ~7 min to **Daikanyama** |
| **6:00 PM** | **Hop Fest, evening session (18:00–20:30)** at **Spring Valley Brewery Tokyo**, Log Road Daikanyama. Up to 20 beers from 18 Japanese breweries, including SVB's fresh-hop **IBUKI** (out Nov 7), sushi and rolls made on site, and the **Kitamura Sisters** playing Tsugaru shamisen this session. Last orders ~8:00 |
| 8:30 PM | Walk to Ebisu: **Ebisu Yokocho** (~20 izakaya stalls under one roof, most open late) or **AFURI Ebisu** yuzu-shio ramen (to 5 AM) |
| ~10:00 PM | Back to Ryogoku: Hibiya Line Ebisu → Akihabara, then Sobu Line (~40 min). Last trains are around midnight |

**Optional, instead of the hotel break:** **Bathe Yotsume Brewery** near Oshiage (weekends from 15:00) for a beer before Hop Fest.

**Not today:** the **Sumo Museum** in the Kokugikan is closed on weekends. No sumo morning practice in Ryogoku either: the November tournament should open today in Fukuoka (it starts on the 2nd Sunday; not checked), so the stables will be there. The Edo-Tokyo Museum's "Toyotomi Brothers!" show (last day today) and the Sumida Hokusai Museum were dropped.

**Hop Fest tickets:** advance **¥3,800** on e+ (admission, a 5-piece sushi set, and four ¥500 vouchers, each good for a 200 ml beer or food), or **¥2,500 at the door** (admission and the four vouchers, no sushi). ~130 advance tickets per session, no cancellations or changes, and door sales can be cut if advance sells out. Payment method at the venue not stated. Seating is free, first come, first served ([bestcalendar](https://bestcalendar.jp/press/159060) · [e+](https://eplus.jp/sf/venue/1504240)).

**Earlier option:** the **14:30–17:00 session** works too. Skip Kameido and leave Ryogoku at ~1:30, then dinner in Ebisu at a normal hour, with the Yebisu Brewery taproom (weekends to 19:00, last entry 18:30) in between.

**Moved off today:** Adam's **Udon Shin**, **Kanoya**, **Shimokitazawa** (Senrogai food trucks) and **Beer Boy** (Shibuya Parco) were on the Oct 23 draft. They need a new day, maybe Tue Nov 10. Udon Shin is open daily and Beer Boy is open to 11:30 PM.
