# Day 13 — Sun Nov 8 — Tokyo (west side: Udon Shin, Shimokitazawa, Shibuya, Ebisu)

**Draft (Oct 23).** Built around Adam's picks: Udon Shin, Senrogai food trucks, Beer Boy and the Yebisu brewery. Sunday suits this side of town because Yebisu Brewery Tokyo is closed Tuesdays, so Nov 10 won't work for it. See Adam's picks under Tokyo in [city-ideas.md](../city-ideas.md).

## Plan
All times are rough estimates.

| Est. time | What |
|---|---|
| 10:00 AM | JR Chuo-Sobu local from Ryogoku runs straight through to **Yoyogi/Shinjuku** (~30 min, no transfer) |
| 10:40 AM | Optional for Adam: a bowl at **Kanoya** (standing soba, Shinjuku south-east exit) |
| 11:00 AM | **Udon Shin** lunch (2-20-16 Yoyogi) at opening. Reserve on TableCheck (¥2,000 pp, non-refundable). Cash only |
| 12:15 PM | Odakyu Line Shinjuku → **Shimokitazawa** (~8 min). Thrift and vintage shops, **Bonus Track**, **Reload**, and the food trucks at **Shimokita Senrogai Open Space** for a snack |
| 3:00 PM | Keio Inokashira Line to Shibuya (~5 min). **Beer Boy** standing bar in Shibuya Parco B1. Nonbei Yokocho is by the station if we skipped it on Oct 30 |
| 4:30 PM | JR Yamanote one stop to **Ebisu**, ~5 min walk to **Yebisu Brewery Tokyo** (Yebisu Garden Place). Weekend hours 11 AM–7 PM (last entry 6:30), no cash. Taproom tastings, plus a tour if one is booked. The **YEBISU Marché** farmers' market is on in the plaza today until 5 PM |
| 6:30 PM | Dinner: **Ebisu Yokocho** (~20 izakaya stalls under one roof) |
| ~9:00 PM | Back to Ryogoku: Hibiya Line Ebisu → Akihabara, then Sobu Line (~40 min) |

**Morning add-on, if up early:** the **Tomioka Hachimangu antique market** (Monzen-Nakacho, 3 stops on the Oedo Line) is on today, the 2nd Sunday, ~8 AM–3 PM. 8:00–9:30, then head west, with Udon Shin at ~11:30.

**Not today:** Akihabara Hokoten (Sun 1–5 PM) and Daikokuyu sento were the other Sunday ideas. Swap Shimokitazawa for them if the group would rather stay east.

## Travel
- Ryogoku → Shinjuku: JR Chuo-Sobu local, direct (~30 min)
- Shinjuku → Shimokitazawa: Odakyu Line (~8 min)
- Shimokitazawa → Shibuya: Keio Inokashira Line (~5 min)
- Shibuya → Ebisu: JR Yamanote (2 min)
- Ebisu → Ryogoku: Hibiya Line to Akihabara + Sobu Line (~40 min)
