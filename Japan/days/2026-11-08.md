# Day 13 — Sun Nov 8 — Tokyo (Ryogoku morning, Shimokitazawa food trucks, Gotokuji, Nakameguro, Hop Fest in Daikanyama, Ebisu late)

**Updated Nov 8 (on the day).** The morning around the hotel is done. The afternoon now goes west for Adam's **Shimokitazawa food trucks** (back on from "Moved off today"): lunch at noon, the maneki-neko cats at **Gotokuji** (before its shop closes at 3:00), a vintage-shop wander, then **Nakameguro** and the Meguro River, walking into Daikanyama for the **Craft Beer Japan Hop Fest** at Spring Valley Brewery (18:00 session) and late bites in Ebisu. Hours are from Google Maps or the neighborhood notes unless marked.

## Plan
All times are rough estimates.

| Est. time | What |
|---|---|
| ✅ Morning | Done: the Ryogoku stops around the hotel (**Kyu-Yasuda Garden**, the **Sumida River walk**, the **Kokugikan** from outside) |
| ~11:15 AM | Ryogoku → Shimokitazawa (~40 min): JR Chuo-Sobu local to Shinjuku, then Odakyu Line ~8 min to Shimokitazawa |
| **12:00 PM** | **Lunch at Shimokita Senrogai Open Space** (Senrogai Akichi), on the old rail line. **Today's trucks (official site, checked Nov 8): only two**, **vantacos** (steak and taco rice) and **juri cafe** (dango), both 11:00–21:00. Also the Akichi Cafe & Bar (11:00–23:00) ([official](https://senrogai.com/akichi/)). Backup if that's too thin: a curry shop in town (Shimokita is a curry neighborhood) |
| 12:45 PM | Odakyu Line 2 stops to **Gotokuji** station, walk ~10 min to **Gotokuji Temple**, where the maneki-neko (beckoning cat) story comes from. Thousands of white cat figurines and a three-story pagoda. ~45 min. The **temple office, which sells the cat figurines, is open 8:00–15:00** ([official](https://gotokuji.jp/en/shop/)), so go right after lunch. The grounds stay open until about 17:00 in this season (listings, not the official site) |
| 2:00 PM | Back to Shimokitazawa (Odakyu, 2 stops). Walk the strip: **reload** (white terraced complex with Ogawa Coffee Laboratory; go up to the top terraces) one way, **Bonus Track** (~9 indie shops around a plaza, including Hakko Department for fermented foods) the other way |
| 2:30 PM | **Thrift and vintage crawl**: the backstreets north of the station (Ichibangai) and south. Cheaper than Harajuku |
| 3:30 PM | Shimokitazawa → Nakameguro (~20 min): Keio Inokashira Line to Shibuya, Tokyu Toyoko Line 2 stops to Nakameguro |
| 4:00 PM | **Nakameguro**: walk along the **Meguro River**, look into the **Koukashita** shops under the tracks, and have one beer at the **Baird Beer Nakameguro Taproom** (GT Plaza C 2F, Sun 12:00–22:00, checked Nov 8, [official](https://bairdbeer.com/pages/nakameguro-taproom)) |
| 5:15 PM | Walk ~10 min uphill to Daikanyama. **Daikanyama T-Site** (design-focused bookstore complex) or the shops on Log Road, then get to the venue early, since seating is first come, first served |
| **6:00 PM** | **Hop Fest, evening session (18:00–20:30)** at **Spring Valley Brewery Tokyo**, Log Road Daikanyama. Up to 20 beers from 18 Japanese breweries, including SVB's fresh-hop **IBUKI** (out Nov 7), sushi and rolls made on site, and the **Kitamura Sisters** playing Tsugaru shamisen this session. Last orders ~8:00 |
| 8:30 PM | Walk to Ebisu: **Ebisu Yokocho** (~20 izakaya stalls under one roof, most open late) or **AFURI Ebisu** yuzu-shio ramen (to 5 AM) |
| ~10:00 PM | Back to Ryogoku: Hibiya Line Ebisu → Akihabara, then Sobu Line (~40 min). Last trains are around midnight |

**Swaps:**
- **More shopping, fewer stops:** skip Gotokuji and stay in Shimokitazawa until ~3:30. It can easily fill the whole afternoon.
- **Beer instead of Nakameguro:** go to **Yebisu Brewery Tokyo** at Ebisu Garden Place (weekends to 19:00, last entry 18:30) around 4:30, then walk ~7 min to Daikanyama. Today the **YEBISU Marché** is also on at Château Plaza (11:00–17:00, 2nd and 4th Sundays, confirmed on the [official](https://gardenplace.jp/event/) events page Nov 8). The Baccarat illumination doesn't start until Nov 14 (also confirmed).

**Dropped today for Shimokitazawa:** brunch at **Ryogoku Edo Noren**, **Ekoin Temple** and the **Kira residence site**, **Kameido Tenjin**'s chrysanthemum festival (runs through Nov 23, so Nov 10 or 11 could still work) and **Kameido Gyoza**. These are all close to the hotel, so they fit easily on another day.

**Not today:** the **Sumo Museum** in the Kokugikan is closed on weekends. No sumo morning practice in Ryogoku either: the November tournament should open today in Fukuoka (it starts on the 2nd Sunday; not checked), so the stables will be there.

**Hop Fest tickets:** advance **¥3,800** on e+ (admission, a 5-piece sushi set, and four ¥500 vouchers, each good for a 200 ml beer or food), or **¥2,500 at the door** (admission and the four vouchers, no sushi). ~130 advance tickets per session, no cancellations or changes, and door sales can be cut if advance sells out. Payment method at the venue not stated. Seating is free, first come, first served ([bestcalendar](https://bestcalendar.jp/press/159060) · [e+](https://eplus.jp/sf/venue/1504240)).

**Still needs a day:** Adam's **Udon Shin** and **Kanoya** (from the Oct 23 draft), maybe Tue Nov 10. Udon Shin is open daily.
