# Day 12 — Sat Nov 7 — Kanazawa → Tokyo (Higashi Chaya, crab lunch at Omicho)

First day local crab is in shops (the first auction was ~7:30 PM last night, Nov 6; rough weather can delay the season opening by a few days, as in 2023). Crab lunch, then an early-afternoon Shinkansen to Tokyo.

## Plan
All times are rough estimates.

| Est. time | What |
|---|---|
| 8:30 AM | Breakfast, pack |
| 9:15 AM | Check out. Put bags in station lockers, or ask the hotel to hold them |
| 9:30 AM | Bus (~10 min, to Hashiba-cho) or taxi to **Higashi Chaya District**. Walk the teahouse streets while they're still quiet, and look in the gold-leaf shops. Step inside **Shima** (an Edo-era geisha house, 9:30 AM–5:30 PM, ¥500). **Try the sweets here:** order the matcha with a seasonal **wagashi** (Kanazawa is one of Japan's big wagashi towns; city-ideas suggests this over the gold-leaf soft serve) |
| ~10:15 AM | **Sake-lees soft serve at Fukumitsuya Higashi** (1-14-9 Higashiyama, 10:00 AM–6:00 PM), the brewery's own shop in an old townhouse. ~15 min. No sake flight this early |
| 10:35 AM | Cross the Asano River (~5 min) to **Kazuemachi**: the quiet riverside geisha lanes (Akarizaka, Kuragarizaka) |
| 10:50 AM | Walk ~15 min to Omicho. (Skipping Oriental Brewing Higashiyama: it opens at 11:00 and would push the crab lunch late. The same brewery's beer is at the station taproom before the train) |
| 11:05 AM | **Crab lunch** on **opening day for local crab**: **Kawaki Shoten** (9:00 AM–3:00 PM; boils crab on site) for **kobako gani** (female, with roe) and **kano gani** (male, blue tag), or a kaisendon topped with kobako. The best stalls sell out, so don't come later than this. Grab ekiben or snacks for the train while you're here |
| 12:30 PM | Walk ~15 min back to the station, grab bags. Or a last pint at **Oriental Brewing Kanazawa Station** (inside the station, 2F, 11:00 AM–10:00 PM) |
| **1:50 PM** | **Kagayaki 534** Kanazawa → Tokyo (~2.5 hrs, all seats reserved) |
| 4:20 PM | Arrive Tokyo Station (confirmed timetable: Ueno 4:14, Tokyo 4:20). JR to Ryogoku, check in |
| ~6:30 PM | **Tori-no-Ichi** fair at **Ootori Shrine** (Senzoku, north of Asakusa). Sobu Line to Akihabara, then Hibiya Line to Iriya (~25–30 min). Runs until midnight, busiest ~7–11 PM. **Dinner: no reservation.** Eat at the fair's food stalls, or grab something quick near the hotel or on the way first, then head up |

**Want to make something?** A **gold-leaf workshop** in Higashi Chaya (~1 hr): **HAKUICHI** or **Kinpakuya Sakuda** (both shops 9:00 AM–5:00 PM; check workshop times and booking when you plan it). It replaces Kazuemachi and pushes the crab lunch to ~11:30. See the gold-leaf entry in [city-ideas.md](../city-ideas.md).

**Decided: afternoon train.** (Skipped: the **Kanazawa Castle + Kenrokuen light-up** is on tonight, but the last Tokyo-bound train is Kagayaki 518 at 9:03 PM, which would put us in Ryogoku around midnight.)

**Decided (Oct 24): the evening is Tori-no-Ichi, with no dinner reservation.** Tonight is the first Tori-no-Ichi of the year (the rooster-day fair). We'll eat at the fair stalls, or find food first and then go. The chanko at Tomoegata is dropped; Ryogoku chanko can fit another night if we want it. See Festivals/Events under Tokyo in [city-ideas.md](../city-ideas.md).

Backups: **Hakutaka 564 at 12:58 PM** or **Hakutaka 566 at 1:58 PM** (slower, ~3 hrs). Saturday in foliage season, so reserve 3 seats together
