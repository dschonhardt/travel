# Day 12 — Sat Nov 7 — Kanazawa → Tokyo (Higashi Chaya + sake flight, crab lunch at Omicho)

First day local crab is in shops (the first auction is ~3 AM today). Crab lunch, then an early-afternoon Shinkansen to Tokyo.

## Plan
All times are rough estimates.

| Est. time | What |
|---|---|
| 8:30 AM | Breakfast, pack |
| 9:15 AM | Check out. Put bags in station lockers, or ask the hotel to hold them |
| 9:30 AM | Bus (~10 min, to Hashiba-cho) or taxi to **Higashi Chaya District**. Walk the teahouse streets while they're still quiet, and look in the gold-leaf shops. Step inside a teahouse like **Shima** (an Edo-era geisha house, ~¥500) if you want an interior |
| ~10:15 AM | **Sake flight at Fukumitsuya Higashi** (1-14-9 Higashiyama), the brewery's own shop in an old townhouse, with a **counter for drinking by the glass**. An old review had 3 sakes for ~¥1,500. Also the **sake-lees soft serve**. Opening time not confirmed; see TODO |
| 10:50 AM | Cross the Asano River (~5 min) to **Kazuemachi**: the quiet riverside geisha lanes (Akarizaka, Kuragarizaka) |
| 11:05 AM | Walk ~15 min to Omicho. (Skipping Oriental Brewing Higashiyama: it opens at 11:00 and would push the crab lunch late. The same brewery's beer is at the station taproom before the train) |
| 11:20 AM | **Crab lunch** on **opening day for local crab**: **Kawaki Shoten** (boils crab on site) for **kobako gani** (female, with roe) and **kano gani** (male, blue tag), or a kaisendon topped with kobako. The best stalls sell out, so don't come later than this. Grab ekiben or snacks for the train while you're here |
| 12:30 PM | Walk ~15 min back to the station, grab bags. Or a last pint at **Oriental Brewing Kanazawa Station** (inside the station, 2F) |
| **1:50 PM** | **Kagayaki 534** Kanazawa → Tokyo (~2.5 hrs, all seats reserved) |
| ~4:20 PM | Arrive Tokyo Station. JR to Ryogoku, check in |
| 7:00 PM | Easy dinner near the hotel. Ryogoku is sumo town, so **chanko nabe** (sumo hot pot) |

**Want to make something?** A **gold-leaf workshop** in Higashi Chaya (~1 hr): **HAKUICHI** at 10:00 (walk-ins if there's space) or **Kinpakuya Sakuda** at 10:30 (book online by 4 PM the day before). It replaces Kazuemachi and pushes the crab lunch to ~11:45. See the gold-leaf entry in [city-ideas.md](../city-ideas.md).

**Decided: afternoon train.** (Skipped: the **Kanazawa Castle + Kenrokuen light-up** is on tonight, 6–9 PM, but the last Tokyo-bound train is Kagayaki 518 at 9:03 PM, which would put us in Ryogoku around midnight.)

**Evening option in Tokyo:** tonight is the first **Tori-no-Ichi** fair at Ootori Shrine (Senzoku, north of Asakusa). It runs until midnight, with a peak of ~7–11 PM. After chanko, take the Sobu Line to Akihabara, then the Hibiya Line to Iriya (~25–30 min). It's another reason to take the afternoon train. See Festivals/Events under Tokyo in [city-ideas.md](../city-ideas.md).

## Travel
- Hokuriku Shinkansen Kanazawa → Tokyo: **Kagayaki 534, 1:50 PM** (~2.5 hrs). It's flagged as "may not run every day," so confirm it runs Sat Nov 7 when booking. Backups: **Hakutaka 564 at 12:58 PM** or **Hakutaka 566 at 1:58 PM** (slower, ~3 hrs). Saturday in foliage season, so reserve 3 seats together
- Tokyo Station → hotel: JR Chuo-Sobu local from Akihabara to Ryogoku (~15–20 min total), or JR Sobu Rapid to Kinshicho and walk
