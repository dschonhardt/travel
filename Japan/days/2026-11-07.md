# Day 12 — Sat Nov 7 — Kanazawa → Tokyo (crab lunch at Omicho)

**Tentative.** First day local crab is in shops (the first auction is ~3 AM today). Crab lunch, then an early-afternoon Shinkansen to Tokyo.

## Plan
All times are rough estimates.

| Est. time | What |
|---|---|
| 8:30 AM | Breakfast, pack |
| 9:30 AM | Check out. Put bags in station lockers, or ask the hotel to hold them |
| 9:45 AM | Bus or taxi to **Higashi Chaya District**: teahouse streets, gold-leaf shops, **gold-leaf ice cream**. Optional short **gold-leaf workshop** if there's time (~45–60 min, book ahead) |
| 11:15 AM | Walk ~15 min to Omicho |
| 11:30 AM | **Omicho Market crab lunch**: **Kawaki Shoten** (boils crab on site) for **kobako gani** (female, with roe) and **kano gani** (male, blue tag), or a kaisendon topped with kobako. Go before the stalls sell out |
| 1:00 PM | Back to the station, grab bags. Ekiben or snacks for the train |
| ~1:30 PM | **Hokuriku Shinkansen** (Kagayaki) Kanazawa → Tokyo, ~2.5–3 hrs |
| ~4:30 PM | Arrive Tokyo Station. JR to Ryogoku, check in |
| 7:00 PM | Easy dinner near the hotel. Ryogoku is sumo town, so **chanko nabe** (sumo hot pot) |

**Late-train option:** stay for the **Kanazawa Castle + Kenrokuen autumn light-up** tonight (free night entry), and take the last Shinkansen to Tokyo. Check the last train first. It means getting to Ryogoku late, so the afternoon plan is the default.

**Evening option in Tokyo:** tonight is the first **Tori-no-Ichi** fair at Ootori Shrine (Senzoku, north of Asakusa). It runs until midnight, with a peak of ~7–11 PM. After chanko, take the Sobu Line to Akihabara, then the Hibiya Line to Iriya (~25–30 min). It's another reason to take the afternoon train. See Festivals/Events under Tokyo in [city-ideas.md](../city-ideas.md).

## Travel
- Hokuriku Shinkansen Kanazawa → Tokyo. Kagayaki is the fastest (~2.5 hrs). Train time and fare not picked yet. Saturday in foliage season, so reserve 3 seats together
- Tokyo Station → hotel: JR Chuo-Sobu local from Akihabara to Ryogoku (~15–20 min total), or JR Sobu Rapid to Kinshicho and walk
