# Day 8 — Tue Nov 3 — Takayama (Kamikochi day trip)

**Note:** Nov 3 is Culture Day (文化の日), a national holiday — expect heavier crowds on buses and at Kamikochi. The buses can't be reserved (first come, first served), so go early and don't cut the return close.

## Plan

**Getting there:** Nohi Bus from Takayama Bus Terminal → Hirayu Onsen (transfer) → Kamikochi, ~1h45 each way. Terminal is right across from JR Takayama Station (~5 min walk from the hotel). Plan is built around the **7:00 AM bus**; 7:40 also works. The 6:00 beats the crowds but arrives before Trois Cinq and Kamonjigoya open, so only take it if you'll start with the walk and eat later. Buses leave Kamikochi every 30 min; the last connection back to Takayama departs 5:00 PM.

**No reservations:** every bus is unreserved and boards in queue order. On a busy day (this is a national holiday) you may have to wait for the next bus.

**Tickets:** buy a **Takayama ↔ Kamikochi round-trip ticket** at the Bus Center counter before boarding. It covers both legs each way (Takayama–Hirayu + Hirayu–Kamikochi shuttle) and is cheaper than leg-by-leg. IC/QR payment accepted; carry cash as backup (can only pay onboard when the counter is closed).

**Numbered return ticket (seiriken):** as soon as you arrive in Kamikochi, go to the window in the **Kamikochi Sightseeing Center** at the bus terminal. Tell them the return bus time and they give you a free numbered ticket — a queue number for that departure, not a seat reservation. On busy days early numbers board first. Get it **before** starting the walk.

Hirayu ↔ Kamikochi shuttle runs through Nov 15, 2026. [Nohi Bus: Kamikochi line](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/route_bus/kamikochi-line-en/) · [Kamikochi official: access from Takayama](https://www.kamikochi.org/gettinghere/access-to-kamikochi-from-takayama) · [Kamikochi Bus Terminal](https://www.kamikochi.org/spot/kamikochi-bus-terminal)

### Schedule

All times are rough estimates, shown for the 7:00 AM bus. Shift them for the 6:00 or 7:40.

| Est. time | What |

| 11:00 AM | **Grilled iwana** (mountain char) at **Kamonjigoya**, next to the pond. Open 10:00 AM–2:00 PM (food served through Nov 10). No reservations, and the iwana sells out, so go straight there on arrival rather than after the pond |

| 5:31 PM | Back in Takayama |

Tel 0577-36-2911. On the way out, buy their **bottled Hida beef side dish** at the counter (good souvenir). Closed Wednesdays, so tonight is the last chance. *Backups:* **Maruaki** (A5 Hida beef, open Tuesdays, ~5 min from the hotel) or anything left from the Day 7 list |
| 7:30 PM | **Sanmachi Suji evening stroll** — quieter after day-trippers leave, and lantern-lit after dark |

**Optional idea: Shinhotaka Ropeway** (not in the plan). A two-stage ropeway, the second stage in Japan's only double-decker gondola, up to a rooftop deck at 2,156 m with a panoramic view of the Northern Alps. It's only worth it on a clear day, because on a cloudy one you ride up into fog. Fitting it in on Nov 3 would mean dropping the Myojin Pond walk: bus Kamikochi → Hirayu Onsen → Shinhotaka (~30 min from Hirayu), then a separate bus back to Takayama. Adds ~2.5 hrs; the ropeway alone is **¥3,800 round trip**. Consider it only if the forecast is clear and you would rather have views than the river walk. Not closed for maintenance on Nov 3 (2026 closures are Jun 15–26 and Nov 24–27). November last ride up is ~4:00 PM (unconfirmed). Shinhotaka → Takayama buses leave 1:46, 2:55, 3:55, 4:55, 5:55 PM ([Nohi Bus Shinhotaka line](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/route_bus/shinhotaka-line/)).

## Foods to try

- **Grilled iwana** (mountain char, wood-fire grilled) — **Kamonjigoya**, Myojin Pond
- **Apple pie** (local Fuji apples) — **Trois Cinq**, Kappa Bridge
- **Sanzoku-yaki** (garlic-soy fried chicken thigh) — **Gosenjaku Kitchen**, Gosenjaku Hotel; or **Taisho-ike Hotel**, Taisho Pond (lunch only, 11 AM–1 PM)
- **Imperial Hotel beef curry** — **Alpenrose**, Kamikochi Imperial Hotel
- **Kamikochi croquette** (Shinshu beef) — **Cafe Konashi**, Hotel Shirakabaso
- **Shinshu soba** (Nagano buckwheat noodles) — **Kamikochi Shokudo**, bus terminal
- **Hone-zake** (hot sake over smoked char) — **Kamonjigoya**, Myojin Pond
- **Rare cheesecake** — **Lounge**, Gosenjaku Hotel
