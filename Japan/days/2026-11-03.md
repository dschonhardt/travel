# Day 8 — Tue Nov 3 — Takayama (Kamikochi day trip)

**Note:** Nov 3 is Culture Day (文化の日), a national holiday — expect heavier crowds on buses and at Kamikochi. Book the Nohi Bus round-trip in advance.

## Travel
- **Nohi Bus: Takayama Bus Terminal → Hirayu Onsen (transfer) → Kamikochi**, ~1h45 each way
- Bus terminal is right across from JR Takayama Station (~5 min walk from the hotel)
- Suggested departure: **7:00 AM or 8:00 AM** — earlier is better for light and crowds
- Return: last bus from Kamikochi typically ~4:30–5:00 PM; confirm exact time when booking
- Round-trip: ~¥4,500–5,000 pp

## Lodging
- **Hida Takayama Washington Hotel Plaza** (booked) — Night 2 of 2. Check-out tomorrow morning

## Plan
All times are rough estimates. Based on a 7:00 AM departure.

| Est. time | What |
|---|---|
| 6:30 AM | Quick breakfast near the hotel before heading to the bus terminal |
| 7:00 AM | **Nohi Bus departs** Takayama Bus Terminal. Transfer at Hirayu Onsen (~1 hr in) |
| 8:45 AM | Arrive **Kamikochi** (上高地) |
| 9:00 AM | **Kappa Bridge** — iconic suspension bridge over the Azusa River with Hotaka peaks behind it; the main landmark and starting point for all trails |
| 9:30 AM | **Azusa River walk north toward Myojin Pond** — ~5 km one-way, completely flat, ~1.5 hrs each way. Best foliage walk in the park; larches along the river corridor turn gold in early November |
| 11:30 AM | Myojin Pond area — turn around or rest before heading back |
| 1:00 PM | **Lunch** at the visitor center area near Kappa Bridge |
| 2:00 PM | **Taisho Pond** — 15–20 min south of Kappa Bridge on foot. Reflection of Yakedake volcano, especially striking with autumn color; quieter than the bridge area |
| 2:30 PM | Optional: **Shinhotaka Ropeway** add-on — bus from Kamikochi back to Hirayu Onsen, then ~30 min to Shinhotaka. Gondola to 2,156 m with full panoramic Alps views. Adds ~2.5 hrs and ¥3,000. Only feasible if skipping the Myojin Pond walk |
| 4:00 PM | **Last bus departs** Kamikochi for Takayama (confirm exact time when booking) |
| 5:45 PM | Back in Takayama |
| 6:30 PM | **Sanmachi Suji evening stroll** — quieter after day-trippers leave |
| 7:30 PM | **Dinner** — anything left from the Day 7 list |
