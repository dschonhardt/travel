# Day 8 — Tue Nov 3 — Takayama (coffee, morning market, two hikes with views)

**Changed Nov 3:** Kamikochi is dropped. Staying in Takayama instead: everything is on foot or ≤10 min by bus from the hotel. (The old Kamikochi plan is in git history.)

**Note:** Nov 3 is Culture Day (文化の日), a national holiday, so expect bigger crowds at the market and in old town. Lots of Takayama places close on Tuesdays (Sakaguchiya, Kyoya, Menya Shirakawa, Heianraku, Blue Penguin), so check Google before walking anywhere for food.

## Plan
All times are rough estimates.

| Est. time | What |
|---|---|
| 7:00 AM | Shower, get ready |
| 7:45 AM | **Latte + snack.** First choice **Falò Coffee Brewers**: house roaster in an old heritage building, bold flat whites, opens **8:00**. Earlier options: **Hids' cafe & bar** (1F of eph TAKAYAMA hotel, Australian specialty espresso, dine-in from ~7:30) or **Coffee 4 no 65** (opens 7:00, few seats). **Coffee Don** in Honmachi is old-school, but reviewers rate its espresso. Hours are from third-party listings; check Google on the holiday |
| 8:30 AM | **Morning markets**: **Jinya-mae** first (in front of Takayama Jinya), then cross the river to **Miyagawa**. Graze: mitarashi dango, pickles, Hida beef bites. Holiday crowds, so be done by ~9:30 when the tour buses arrive |
| 9:45 AM | **Hike 1: Higashiyama Walking Course → Shiroyama Park.** Starts ~10 min walk east of the market, no transit. ~3.5 km through the temple and shrine district, ending at the **Takayama Castle ruins** in Shiroyama Park. Early November is peak foliage, with views over town to the mountains. ~1.5–2 hrs. Signed, with restrooms and water along the way. Comes out by old town |
| 12:00 PM | **Light lunch** in old town: **Kajibashi** (small bowl of classic Takayama chuka soba, walk-in, usually no line) or **Menya Toto** (chicken + sardine broth, near the Miyagawa market). Check Tuesday hours first |
| 1:15 PM | **Bus to Hida no Sato** (Hida Folk Village) from the Nohi Bus Center across from the hotel, ~10 min. Suica/IC works |
| 1:30 PM | **Hike 2: Matsukura Castle ruins** (松倉城跡, Matsukura-yama). ~20 min walk from the Hida no Sato stop to the trail, then a short, steep forest climb to the summit stone walls. **Best view of the day**: Takayama with the Northern Alps behind. It's in the afternoon because the Alps are east of town, so the sun is behind you and lights the peaks. *Longer option:* walk back down into town instead of taking the bus (the full loop from the station is ~10 km, ~260 m of climbing) |
| ~4:00 PM | **Be off the mountain by ~4:00** (sunset ~4:50, and bears are most active at dusk). Bus back, or stop at Hida Folk Village if there's time (closes 5:00) |
| 5:00 PM | Hotel: shower, rest. The hotel has an onsen bath |
| 6:00 PM | **Dinner at Kitchen Hida**: casual Hida beef steak sets, 1-66 Hon-machi, a short walk from Sanmachi Suji. Dinner 5:00–8:30 PM, **last order 7:45 PM**. Tel 0577-36-2911. On the way out, buy their **bottled Hida beef side dish** at the counter (good souvenir). Closed Wednesdays, so tonight is the last chance. *Backup:* **Maruaki** (A5 Hida beef, open Tuesdays, ~5 min from the hotel) |
| 7:30 PM | **Sanmachi Suji evening stroll**: quieter after the day-trippers leave, and lantern-lit after dark |

**Bears:** bears have been seen in Shiroyama Park, and both hikes go through forest. Carry a **bear bell** (sold in town), stay on the trail, and don't hike at dusk.

**If it's cloudy:** swap the hikes. Do Matsukura in the morning (bus right after the market), then the Higashiyama temple walk in the afternoon, where the view matters less.

**Tomorrow:** the market is done today, so the Nov 4 morning can be a later, slower start (Showa-kan, then the 11:03 train).
