# Day 8 — Tue Nov 3 — Takayama (Kamikochi day trip)

**Note:** Nov 3 is Culture Day (文化の日), a national holiday — expect heavier crowds on buses and at Kamikochi. The buses can't be reserved (first come, first served), so go early and don't cut the return close.

## Travel
- **Nohi Bus: Takayama Bus Terminal → Hirayu Onsen (transfer) → Kamikochi**, ~1h45 each way
- Bus terminal is right across from JR Takayama Station (~5 min walk from the hotel)
- Departure: pick a 6:00, 7:00, or 7:40 AM bus that morning (see bus options below). Earlier means fewer holiday crowds
- Return: buses leave Kamikochi every 30 min. The last one that connects back to Takayama is ~5:00 PM; check the exact time at the Bus Center
- **No reservations:** every bus on this route is unreserved, and you board in the order you line up. On a busy day you may have to wait for the next bus
- **Tickets:** buy at the counter or ticket machine at the **Takayama Nohi Bus Center** before boarding (IC/QR payment was added there in 2021). Don't count on tapping Suica on the bus itself. Carry cash as a backup, since you can only pay onboard when the counter is closed
- **Round-trip ticket:** buy a **Takayama ↔ Kamikochi round trip** at the Bus Center counter. It covers both buses each way (Takayama–Hirayu and the Hirayu–Kamikochi shuttle), so there's nothing to buy along the way, and it's cheaper than paying leg by leg. Valid ~6–7 days. One-way is ¥3,000 for reference
- **Numbered return ticket (seiriken):** as soon as you get off the bus in Kamikochi, go to the window in the **Kamikochi Sightseeing Center** building at the bus terminal. Tell them the return bus time you want and they give you a free numbered ticket. It's a queue number for that departure, not a seat reservation. On busy days, especially for the last buses, people holding numbered tickets board first, and early numbers go fast. Get it **before** starting the walk
- Hirayu ↔ Kamikochi shuttle runs through Nov 15, 2026. Takayama ↔ Hirayu runs year-round
- [Nohi Bus: Kamikochi line](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/route_bus/kamikochi-line-en/) · [Kamikochi official: access from Takayama](https://www.kamikochi.org/gettinghere/access-to-kamikochi-from-takayama) · [Kamikochi Bus Terminal](https://www.kamikochi.org/spot/kamikochi-bus-terminal)

Recheck 2026 times at the Bus Center. Kamikochi arrivals assume the next shuttle after reaching Hirayu (~25 min ride).

| Leave Kamikochi | Arrive Takayama | Notes |

## Plan
All times are rough estimates, shown for the 7:00 AM bus. Shift them for the 6:00 or 7:40.

| Est. time | What |
|---|---|
| 6:30 AM | Quick breakfast near the hotel before heading to the bus terminal |
| 7:00 AM | **Nohi Bus departs** Takayama Bus Terminal. Transfer at Hirayu Onsen (~1 hr in) |
| ~8:25–8:55 AM | Arrive **Kamikochi** (上高地), depending on the Hirayu shuttle connection. **First stop: get a numbered return ticket** for the 4:00 PM bus at the Kamikochi Sightseeing Center window |
| 9:00 AM | **Kappa Bridge** — iconic suspension bridge over the Azusa River with Hotaka peaks behind it; the main landmark and starting point for all trails |
| 9:15 AM | **Apple pie** from **Trois Cinq**, by Kappa Bridge. Take it on the walk |
| 9:30 AM | **Azusa River walk north toward Myojin Pond** — ~5 km one-way, completely flat, ~1.5 hrs each way. Best foliage walk in the park; larches along the river corridor turn gold in early November |
| 11:30 AM | **Myojin Pond** area. Rest before heading back |
| 11:45 AM | **Grilled iwana** (mountain char) at **Kamonjigoya**, next to the pond |
| 1:00 PM | **Lunch** near Kappa Bridge. Options: **Gosenjaku Kitchen** (sanzoku-yaki set), **Alpenrose** at the Imperial Hotel (beef curry, the splurge), or **Cafe Konashi** (Kamikochi croquette, quick and cheap) |
| 2:00 PM | **Taisho Pond** — 15–20 min south of Kappa Bridge on foot. Reflection of Yakedake volcano, especially striking with autumn color; quieter than the bridge area |
| 4:00 PM | **Bus back** from Kamikochi. The last connection to Takayama is ~5:00 PM; check at the Bus Center |
| ~5:30 PM | Back in Takayama |
| 6:30 PM | **Sanmachi Suji evening stroll** — quieter after day-trippers leave |
| 7:30 PM | **Dinner** — anything left from the Day 7 list |

**Optional idea: Shinhotaka Ropeway** (not in the plan). A two-stage ropeway, the second stage in Japan's only double-decker gondola, up to a rooftop deck at 2,156 m with a panoramic view of the Northern Alps. It's only worth it on a clear day, because on a cloudy one you ride up into fog. Fitting it in on Nov 3 would mean dropping the Myojin Pond walk: bus Kamikochi → Hirayu Onsen → Shinhotaka (~30 min from Hirayu), then a separate bus back to Takayama. Adds ~2.5 hrs and ~¥3,000. Consider it only if the forecast is clear and you would rather have views than the river walk. If so, check the ropeway's last November ride up and the Shinhotaka → Takayama bus times.

## Foods to try

- **Grilled iwana** (mountain char, wood-fire grilled) — **Kamonjigoya**, Myojin Pond
- **Apple pie** (local Fuji apples) — **Trois Cinq**, Kappa Bridge
- **Sanzoku-yaki** (garlic-soy fried chicken thigh) — **Gosenjaku Kitchen**, Gosenjaku Hotel; or **Taisho-ike Hotel**, Taisho Pond
- **Imperial Hotel beef curry** — **Alpenrose**, Kamikochi Imperial Hotel
- **Kamikochi croquette** (Shinshu beef) — **Cafe Konashi**, Hotel Shirakabaso
- **Shinshu soba** (Nagano buckwheat noodles) — **Kamikochi Shokudo**, bus terminal
- **Hone-zake** (hot sake over smoked char) — **Kamonjigoya**, Myojin Pond
- **Rare cheesecake** — **Lounge**, Gosenjaku Hotel
