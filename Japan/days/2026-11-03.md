# Day 8 — Tue Nov 3 — Takayama (Kamikochi day trip)

**Note:** Nov 3 is Culture Day (文化の日), a national holiday — expect heavier crowds on buses and at Kamikochi. The buses can't be reserved (first come, first served), so go early and don't cut the return close.

## Plan

**Getting there:** Nohi Bus from Takayama Bus Terminal → Hirayu Onsen (transfer) → Kamikochi, ~1h45 each way. Terminal is right across from JR Takayama Station (~5 min walk from the hotel). Pick a 6:00, 7:00, or 7:40 AM bus — earlier means fewer holiday crowds. Buses leave Kamikochi every 30 min; the last connection back to Takayama departs ~5:00 PM.

**No reservations:** every bus is unreserved and boards in queue order. On a busy day (this is a national holiday) you may have to wait for the next bus.

**Tickets:** buy a **Takayama ↔ Kamikochi round-trip ticket** at the Bus Center counter before boarding. It covers both legs each way (Takayama–Hirayu + Hirayu–Kamikochi shuttle) and is cheaper than leg-by-leg. IC/QR payment accepted; carry cash as backup (can only pay onboard when the counter is closed).

**Numbered return ticket (seiriken):** as soon as you arrive in Kamikochi, go to the window in the **Kamikochi Sightseeing Center** at the bus terminal. Tell them the return bus time and they give you a free numbered ticket — a queue number for that departure, not a seat reservation. On busy days early numbers board first. Get it **before** starting the walk.

Hirayu ↔ Kamikochi shuttle runs through Nov 15, 2026. [Nohi Bus: Kamikochi line](https://www.nouhibus.co.jp/route_bus/kamikochi-line-en/) · [Kamikochi official: access from Takayama](https://www.kamikochi.org/gettinghere/access-to-kamikochi-from-takayama) · [Kamikochi Bus Terminal](https://www.kamikochi.org/spot/kamikochi-bus-terminal)

### Bus options

Based on the 2025 season timetable ([Snow Monkey Resorts](https://www.snowmonkeyresorts.com/access/local-bus-timetable-from-takayama-to-hirayu-onsen-kamikochi/)). Recheck 2026 times at the Bus Center. Kamikochi arrivals assume the next shuttle after reaching Hirayu (~25 min ride).

**Afternoon, Kamikochi → Takayama:**

| Leave Kamikochi | Arrive Takayama | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 3:00 PM | ~4:31 PM | Safe choice on a holiday |

Departures leave Kamikochi at :00 and :30. A :00 bus makes a quick connection at Hirayu. A :30 bus waits ~35 min at Hirayu, so it gets to Takayama about the same time as the next :00 bus.

### Schedule

All times are rough estimates, shown for the 7:00 AM bus. Shift them for the 6:00 or 7:40.

| Est. time | What |

| 2:00 PM | **Taisho Pond** — 15–20 min south of Kappa Bridge on foot. Reflection of Yakedake volcano, especially striking with autumn color; quieter than the bridge area |

| ~5:30 PM | Back in Takayama |
| 6:30 PM | **Sanmachi Suji evening stroll** — quieter after day-trippers leave |
| 7:30 PM | **Dinner** — anything left from the Day 7 list |

**Optional idea: Shinhotaka Ropeway** (not in the plan). A two-stage ropeway, the second stage in Japan's only double-decker gondola, up to a rooftop deck at 2,156 m with a panoramic view of the Northern Alps. It's only worth it on a clear day, because on a cloudy one you ride up into fog. Fitting it in on Nov 3 would mean dropping the Myojin Pond walk: bus Kamikochi → Hirayu Onsen → Shinhotaka (~30 min from Hirayu), then a separate bus back to Takayama. Adds ~2.5 hrs and ~¥3,000. Consider it only if the forecast is clear and you would rather have views than the river walk. If so, check the ropeway's last November ride up and the Shinhotaka → Takayama bus times.

## Foods to try

- **Grilled iwana** (mountain char, wood-fire grilled) — **Kamonjigoya**, Myojin Pond
- **Apple pie** (local Fuji apples) — **Trois Cinq**, Kappa Bridge
- **Sanzoku-yaki** (garlic-soy fried chicken thigh) — **Gosenjaku Kitchen**, Gosenjaku Hotel; or **Taisho-ike Hotel**, Taisho Pond
- **Imperial Hotel beef curry** — **Alpenrose**, Kamikochi Imperial Hotel
- **Kamikochi croquette** (Shinshu beef) — **Cafe Konashi**, Hotel Shirakabaso
- **Shinshu soba** (Nagano buckwheat noodles) — **Kamikochi Shokudo**, bus terminal
- **Hone-zake** (hot sake over smoked char) — **Kamonjigoya**, Myojin Pond
- **Rare cheesecake** — **Lounge**, Gosenjaku Hotel
