# Day 2 — Wed Oct 28 — Tokyo (gyudon, capybaras, Asakusa, Akihabara)

## Travel
- Hotel → **Oshiage** (Skytree): Toei Asakusa Line straight from Asakusabashi, ~10 min, no transfers
- Oshiage → **Asakusa**: walk ~20 min across the Sumida River (Azuma Bridge), or 1 stop on the Toei Asakusa Line
- Asakusa → **Akihabara**: Tsukuba Express, ~5 min (TX Asakusa Station is ~5 min walk west of Senso-ji)

## Lodging
- **Hotel LiVEMAX Asakusabashi-Ekimae** (booked) — 1-1-14 Asakusabashi, Taito, Tokyo 111-0053
- Night 2 of 4

## Plan
All times are rough estimates.

**Capybara cafe swap:** the one on the Google Map, [cafe capyba ASAKUSA](https://cspace.co.jp/en/our-shop_english/) (1-1-4 Senzoku), is **closed Wednesdays and Thursdays**. Its only open days in our Tokyo block are Tue Oct 27 (arrival) and Fri Oct 30 (Kamakura), so it doesn't fit. [CAPPINESS Oshiage](https://cappiness.jp/) is open daily, is one direct train from the hotel, and sits across the river from Asakusa.

| Est. time | What |
|---|---|
| 9:00 AM | **Gyudon breakfast** near the hotel: **Matsuya Asakusabashi** (24 hrs, 1-13-4 Yanagibashi) or **Yoshinoya Asakusabashi East Exit** (1-30-1 Asakusabashi, ~1 min from the station) |
| 9:50 AM | Toei Asakusa Line to Oshiage (~10 min) |
| 10:15 AM | **CAPPINESS Oshiage** capybara cafe: 40-min session, ¥4,000 pp, includes drinks, capybara snacks for feeding, a group photo, and a small souvenir. Reservation required |
| 11:00 AM | Walk ~20 min across Azuma Bridge to Asakusa. Good views back to Skytree and the Asahi "golden flame" building |
| 11:30 AM | **Senso-ji**: Kaminarimon gate, **Nakamise Dori** snack stalls, the main hall and five-story pagoda. Wander Denboin-dori and the side streets |
| 12:45 PM | **Lunch in Asakusa**: **Hoppy Street** stew (nikomi) and beer at the open-air izakaya, or tempura, a classic Asakusa lunch |
| 2:15 PM | Tsukuba Express Asakusa → Akihabara (~5 min) |
| 2:30 PM | **Explore Akihabara**: arcades, anime and electronics shops, side streets |
| 5:30 PM | Beer at **Hitachino Brewing Lab** (mAAch ecute Kanda Manseibashi, 1-25-4 Kanda Sudacho): Kiuchi Brewery's taproom in the old brick railway arches by the Kanda River, ~5–10 min walk south of Akihabara Electric Town. Open Mon–Sat 11:00 AM–10:00 PM |
| 7:00 PM | Dinner at **Yuzu Lab Noodles** (2-8 Kanda Sudacho), a few minutes' walk from Hitachino. Open 11:00 AM–10:00 PM (last order 9:30), closed Sundays |
