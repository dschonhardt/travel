## Optional morning run (~30 min, ~4.5–5 km)
Loop on the **Sumida River Terrace** (the riverside path along both banks), starting from the hotel:
1. Hotel → walk/jog ~5 min east to the river at **Yanagibashi**, where the Kanda River meets the Sumida
2. Cross **Ryogoku Bridge** to the east bank and run north on the terrace
3. Keep going past Kuramae Bridge to **Komagata Bridge** (Asakusa side). Good view of Skytree and the Asahi "golden flame" building
4. Cross back to the west bank and run south on the terrace back to Yanagibashi and the hotel

Shorter version (~20–25 min): turn around at **Kuramae Bridge** instead. Parts of the terrace can be closed for construction. If you hit a closure, go up to the street that runs alongside the river and drop back down after it.

## Travel
- Hotel → **Oshiage** (Skytree): Toei Asakusa Line straight from Asakusabashi, ~10 min, no transfers
- Oshiage → **Asakusa**: walk ~20 min across the Sumida River (Azuma Bridge), or 1 stop on the Toei Asakusa Line
