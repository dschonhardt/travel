# Day 6 — Sun Nov 1 — Nagoya (castle, Nagashima Zombie Island, Nabana no Sato lights)

**Tentative.** Last full day with all four of us. Halloween night runs late, so the morning starts easy.

## Plan
All times are rough estimates.

| Est. time | What |
|---|---|
| 9:00 AM | Easy breakfast near the hotel (Komeda's Coffee is the classic Nagoya "morning set") |
| 9:30 AM | **Nagoya Castle**, ~10 min walk from the hotel. ¥1,000, includes the **Honmaru Palace** (the main keep is still closed). The **chrysanthemum festival** (late Oct–late Nov) is on the grounds. Keep it to ~1.5 hrs |
| ~11:15 AM | Quick lunch near Nagoya Station / the Meitetsu Bus Center |
| ~12:00 PM | **Direct bus** to Nagashima Onsen (~50–55 min) |
| 1:00 PM | **Nagashima Zombie Island** at Nagashima Spa Land: zombies roaming the park, the horror street, the Party Dome, and the free youth dance festival at 1:00. **Book a haunted house (Lv. ZOMBIE) time slot as soon as you arrive** |
| 5:00 PM | Park closes. Mie Kotsu bus to **Nabana no Sato** (15–20 min, ¥450) |
| 5:30 PM | **Nabana no Sato illumination**: the gold light tunnel and the big light show. **Group dinner there** with the ¥1,000 food voucher that comes with the ticket |
| ~8:30 PM | Bus to Kintetsu Nagashima Station, Kintetsu train back to Nagoya, subway to the hotel |
| ~9:30 PM | Back at the hotel. **Pack tonight**: check-out is before 10:00 AM and the Hida Express leaves at 9:39 |

**Costs (per person, approx.):** Spa Land admission only ¥1,800 (the ¥6,000 ride pass isn't needed for the zombie events) + haunted house ¥1,000 + Nabana no Sato ~¥2,500 (last season's price, includes the ¥1,000 voucher) + buses/trains.

**Spa Land closes at 5:00 PM on Nov 1** (it's open until 7:30 PM on Oct 31). The event page lists the haunted house as 1:00–7:00 PM, but on Sunday it will almost certainly end when the park closes, so get a slot early.

**If running behind:** cut the castle short. The zombie activity mostly starts at 1:00.

**Alternatives (if the group would rather):**
- **Tōshun sake brewery tour** instead of Zombie Island: 1:30–4:30 PM, meets at JR Shin-Moriyama (~15 min from Nagoya Station). ¥18,000 pp, 3 tastings and a bottle to take home. Book at least a week ahead (by ~Oct 25), and ask first whether it runs on Sundays. Nabana no Sato still works after it
- **Lighter day:** castle and sake tour, then a sit-down group dinner in Sakae (miso oden, Chita beef sushi, or more tebasaki)

## Travel
- Hotel → Nagoya Station: Sakuradori subway from Marunouchi, 2 stops (~5 min)
- **Out (Nagoya → Spa Land):** direct bus from the **Meitetsu Bus Center** (4F, platform 22) to Nagashima Onsen, ~50–55 min, ~¥1,200–1,400. One seat, no transfers
- **Spa Land → Nabana no Sato:** Mie Kotsu bus, 15–20 min, ¥450, runs until ~8:50 PM
- **Back (Nabana no Sato → hotel):** bus to **Kintetsu Nagashima Station** (last bus ~11:00 PM on weekends), then the Kintetsu Nagoya Line semi-express (準急) to **Kintetsu Nagoya**, ~22 min. Then 2 subway stops to Marunouchi. Kintetsu has its own station under Nagoya Station, next to Meitetsu (not the JR side)
- Suica works on the subway, Kintetsu, and Mie Kotsu buses

## Lodging
- **Hotel LiVEMAX PREMIUM Nagoya Marunouchi** (booked) — 3 Chome-16-22 Marunouchi, Naka Ward, Nagoya, Aichi 460-0002
- Night 2 of 2. Check-out is Mon Nov 2 before 10:00 AM

## Foods to try
- **Nabana no Sato dinner** with the food voucher
- **Hitsumabushi** (grilled eel over rice, Nagoya's signature dish) doesn't fit today's quick lunch. Fit it in somewhere else in Nagoya if you can (Atsuta Horaiken has a branch in Matsuzakaya, Sakae)
