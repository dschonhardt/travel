# Day 6 — Sun Nov 1 — Nagoya (castle, afternoon open)

**Tentative.** Last full day with all four of us. Halloween night runs late, so the morning starts easy. Nagashima Zombie Island is dropped. The afternoon and evening are open.

## Plan
All times are rough estimates.

| Est. time | What |
|---|---|
| 9:00 AM | Easy breakfast near the hotel (Komeda's Coffee is the classic Nagoya "morning set") |
| 9:30 AM | **Nagoya Castle**, ~10 min walk from the hotel. ¥1,000, includes the **Honmaru Palace** (the main keep is still closed). The **chrysanthemum festival** (late Oct–late Nov) is on the grounds. Keep it to ~1.5 hrs |
| ~11:15 AM | Lunch (not picked yet) |
| Afternoon | _Open (TBD)_ |
| Evening | _Open (TBD)_ |
| ~9:30 PM | Back at the hotel. **Pack tonight**: check-out is before 10:00 AM and the Hida Express leaves at 9:39 |

**Options for the open afternoon and evening:**
- **Tōshun sake brewery tour**: 1:30–4:30 PM, meets at JR Shin-Moriyama (~15 min from Nagoya Station). ¥18,000 pp, 3 tastings and a bottle to take home. Book at least a week ahead (by ~Oct 25), and ask first whether it runs on Sundays
- **Nabana no Sato illumination** (evening): the gold light tunnel and the big light show. ~¥2,500 (last season's price), includes a ¥1,000 food voucher, so it can double as group dinner. Getting there: Kintetsu Nagoya Line to **Kintetsu Nagashima** (~22 min), then the bus to Nabana no Sato
- **Lighter day:** a sit-down group dinner in Sakae (miso oden, Chita beef sushi, or more tebasaki)

## Travel
- Hotel → Nagoya Castle: on foot, ~10 min
- Hotel → Nagoya Station: Sakuradori subway from Marunouchi, 2 stops (~5 min)
- If doing Nabana no Sato: Kintetsu has its own station under Nagoya Station, next to Meitetsu (not the JR side). The last bus from Nabana no Sato to Kintetsu Nagashima is ~11:00 PM on weekends
- Suica works on the subway, Kintetsu, and Mie Kotsu buses

## Lodging
- **Hotel LiVEMAX PREMIUM Nagoya Marunouchi** (booked) — 3 Chome-16-22 Marunouchi, Naka Ward, Nagoya, Aichi 460-0002
- Night 2 of 2. Check-out is Mon Nov 2 before 10:00 AM

## Foods to try
- **Hitsumabushi** (grilled eel over rice, Nagoya's signature dish). Fit it in today or somewhere else in Nagoya (Atsuta Horaiken has a branch in Matsuzakaya, Sakae)
