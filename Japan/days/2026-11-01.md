# Day 6 — Sun Nov 1 — Nagoya (castle, Atsuta Jingu, Nabana no Sato illumination)

Last full day with all four of us. Halloween night runs late, so the morning starts easy. Nagashima Zombie Island is dropped. **Evening is Nabana no Sato** (decided), probably **Dan solo**: the others are likely heading to one of the museums (Toyota Commemorative Museum or SCMAGLEV, not picked). Afternoon is **Atsuta Jingu**, then an early **hitsumabushi** (eel) dinner at Nagoya Station on the way to Nabana.

## Plan
All times are rough estimates.

| Est. time | What |
|---|---|
| 9:00 AM | Easy breakfast near the hotel (Komeda's Coffee is the classic Nagoya "morning set") |
| 9:30 AM | **Nagoya Castle**, ~15 min walk from the hotel (or Meijo Line 1 stop, Hisaya-odori → Nagoyajo). ¥1,000, includes the **Honmaru Palace** (the main keep is still closed). The **chrysanthemum festival** (late Oct–late Nov) is on the grounds. Keep it to ~1.5 hrs |
| ~11:15 AM | Lunch: **miso nikomi udon** at **Yamamotoya Sohonke, Kinshachi Yokocho** (right outside the castle's main gate, Yoshinao zone). Thick udon cooked in a clay pot of Hatcho-blend red miso broth; the noodles are firm on purpose. 10:30 AM–5:30 PM (last order 5:00), open daily |
| ~12:15 PM | Optional rest at the hotel (~15 min walk from the castle) after the late Halloween night |
| ~1:30 PM | Meijo Line from **Hisaya-odori** (counter-clockwise via Sakae and Kanayama) to **Atsuta Jingu Nishi**, 7 stops (~15 min), then ~7 min walk to the shrine. If skipping the hotel rest, board at Nagoyajo instead (~20 min) |
| ~2:00 PM | **Atsuta Jingu**: grounds are free. Walk out to the South Gate and the main approach, under the giant camphor trees. Optional **Kusanagi-kan** sword museum (¥500, last entry 4:00 PM) |
| ~3:15 PM | Walk ~3 min to Meitetsu **Jingu-mae**, then Meitetsu to Nagoya Station (~6 min). Walk through the station to **Esca**, the underground mall on the west (Taiko-dori) side (~10 min) |
| ~3:40 PM | **Hitsumabushi at Hitsumabushi Ino** (Esca, Nagoya Station): grilled eel over rice, eaten three ways (plain, with condiments, then with broth). 11:00 AM–9:30 PM with no afternoon break. Mini bowls available, handy since lunch was at 11:15. ~45 min |
| ~4:30 PM | Walk back east through the station to **Kintetsu Nagoya** (~10 min; it's next to Meitetsu, not on the JR side). At the Kintetsu limited-express ticket window, buy the **Nabana no Sato illumination set ticket** (なばなの里 イルミネーション セット券, **¥3,670**): round-trip Kintetsu train + bus, admission and the ¥1,000 voucher. Allow ~5 min |
| ~4:45 PM | **Kintetsu Nagoya Line** to **Kintetsu Nagashima** (~22 min; weekend expresses stop there from Oct 31, check the board), then the direct bus to Nabana no Sato (~10 min) |
| ~5:30 PM | **Nabana no Sato illumination**: lights are on by now (sunset ~4:58 PM). Gold light tunnel, the big light show, the flower gardens. Admission is covered by the set ticket and includes a ¥1,000 food voucher. Use it on snacks and drinks, since dinner was the eel. Open until 10:00 PM on Sundays |
| ~8:30 PM | Bus back to Kintetsu Nagashima, then Kintetsu to Nagoya. **The last bus is ~10:00 PM on Sundays** (Nabana closes at 10; the later buses only run on 11 PM nights), so there's still slack |
| ~9:30 PM | Back at the hotel. **Pack tonight**: check-out is before 10:00 AM and the Hida Express leaves at 9:39 |

**Afternoon:** Atsuta Jingu (decided). It sits just south of Nagoya Station, so it's a short detour on the way to Nabana. More detail in [city-ideas.md](../city-ideas.md). Dropped: the Tōshun sake tour (it ends at 4:30, too late for Nabana).

**Rain backup** (in place of Nabana), or if everyone ends up together for the last night: a sit-down group dinner in Sakae. Best fit: **Chita beef** at **Chita-gyu Yakiniku to Basashi Niji** (知多牛焼肉と馬肉 虹), 4-21-17 Sakae, 1F Elizabeth Garden Bldg, ~8 min from Yaba-cho Station. A5 Chita beef yakiniku; the signature is the **sukiyaki-style Chita beef cheek** (¥1,078, with raw egg). Chita beef course ¥4,500 with 2 hrs of all-you-can-drink. 6:00 PM–4:00 AM, **closed Wednesdays** (Sunday is fine). Book on Hot Pepper

## Travel
- Hotel → Nagoya Castle: on foot, ~15 min (the hotel is by **Hisaya-odori** Station, ~1 km from the East Gate), or Meijo Line 1 stop to Nagoyajo
- Hotel → **Atsuta Jingu**: Meijo Line from Hisaya-odori to Atsuta Jingu Nishi, 7 stops (~15 min). From the castle, board at Nagoyajo (~20 min)
- Atsuta Jingu → **Nagoya Station**: Meitetsu from Jingu-mae (~3 min walk from the shrine), ~6 min
- Hotel → Nagoya Station: Sakuradori subway from **Hisaya-odori**, 3 stops (~6 min)
- If doing Nabana no Sato: Kintetsu has its own station under Nagoya Station, next to Meitetsu (not the JR side). Buy the ¥3,670 set ticket there (see the Plan); it's ¥310 cheaper than separate tickets and skips the Nabana ticket window, which can take 30+ min at peak. The last bus from Nabana no Sato to Kintetsu Nagashima is **~10:00 PM on Sundays**
- Suica works on the subway, Kintetsu, and Mie Kotsu buses

## Foods to try
- **Miso nikomi udon** (Hatcho miso): Yamamotoya Sohonke at the castle, lunch today. Other branches: Matsuzakaya 9F in Sakae (11 AM–9 PM) and JR Central Towers 13F at Nagoya Station (11 AM–11 PM). The original Honke in Sakae is closed Tue/Wed
- **Chita beef** (知多牛): branded beef from the Chita Peninsula south of Nagoya. Niji in Sakae (see the dinner option above) is the pick. The official Chita beef site lists other Nagoya restaurants that serve it
- **Uiro to take home**: Osu Uiro (bought on Oct 31) or the station souvenir shops at Nagoya Station before the Hida Express on Nov 2
- **Miso oden**: no good Sunday spot found in Sakae. Order it or doteni if an izakaya has it
- **Hitsumabushi** (grilled eel over rice, Nagoya's signature dish): **Hitsumabushi Ino** in Esca, ~3:40 PM today, between Atsuta Jingu and Nabana. Atsuta Horaiken (the famous original, by the shrine) was skipped: its lunch service ends ~2:00–2:30 PM and it doesn't reopen until 4:30. Backup at the station: **Hitsumabushi Bincho** (Esca and Dai Nagoya Building), but it closes 3:30–5:00 PM. Hours are from listings, not confirmed
