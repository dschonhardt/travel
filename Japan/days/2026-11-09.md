# Day 14 — Mon Nov 9 — Tokyo (Chuo Line: Nakano, Koenji, Kichijoji)

Swapped with Tsukuba (now [Tue Nov 10](./2026-11-10.md)). **One sweep west on the Chuo-Sobu local** (Iidabashi → Nakano → Koenji → Kichijoji), then dinner in Nakano on the ride home. No transfers. Fish Center dropped for rain. Monday hours checked (table below).

**Rain:** 60–80% all day, high 11°C. Bring umbrellas. Nakano Broadway and the Koenji arcades are covered; cut the garden and park short if it's pouring.

**Shorter option:** skip Akiyoshi and eat dinner at Iseya instead. Then you end in Kichijoji and ride straight home (~45 min).

**Left out:** the **Ichigaya Fish Center** (dropped Nov 9 for rain), the **Kosugi-yu** sento in Koenji (skipped Oct 25), Kichijoji's **Beer Boy** (Google Maps has no listing for it, so it may have closed; the Shibuya branch is on [Oct 30](./2026-10-30.md)) and the **Ghibli Museum** (closed Nov 4–18). The **Edo-Tokyo Museum** and the **Sumida Hokusai Museum** by the hotel are both **closed Mondays**. Udon Shin and Kanoya would suit Jeff and Adam on Tue Nov 10 while Dan is on Mt. Tsukuba.

## Travel
- Ryogoku → Iidabashi: JR Chuo-Sobu local, direct (~15 min). Iidabashi → Nakano: Chuo-Sobu local, direct (~15 min)
- Nakano Station → Nogata Hope Nakano branch: up the Sun Mall and through Broadway, ~2–3 min past its north end. Then Nakano → Koenji: JR Chuo Line, 1 stop (~2 min)
- Koenji → Kichijoji → Nakano: Chuo Line, ~10 min each way
- Nakano → Ryogoku: JR Chuo-Sobu local, direct (~35 min). Last trains are around midnight
