# Day 14 — Mon Nov 9 — Tokyo (Mt. Tsukuba day trip)

**Draft.** Mt. Tsukuba replaces Nikko as the second day trip (Nikko is too far). A weekday was picked on purpose, because fall weekends on the mountain are crowded.

## Travel
- Hotel → **Akihabara**: walk ~5 min to JR Ryogoku, then the Chuo-Sobu local two stops (~5 min)
- **Tsukuba Express (TX) Rapid**, Akihabara → **Tsukuba**: ~45 min, ~¥1,200–1,280 one-way
- **Mt. Tsukuba Shuttle Bus** (Kanto Tetsudo), from bus stop 1 at **Tsukuba Center** (outside Tsukuba Station) to **Tsukubasan-jinja-iriguchi** (Tsukuba Shrine Entrance): ~38 min, ¥770. The bus continues to Tsutsujigaoka (¥930)
- Weekday buses from Tsukuba Center (NAVITIME, may be out of date): **8:00, 8:50, 9:40, 10:20, 11:00, 11:50, 13:30, 14:15, 15:00**
- **Last weekday bus back** from the Shrine Entrance is about **5:10 PM**. Don't miss it
- **Buy the Mt. Tsukuba Ticket (筑波山きっぷ)** from the TX ticket machine at Akihabara (it isn't sold at Tsukuba Station): **¥4,670 from Akihabara** at last check (may have changed with the March 2026 TX fare revision). Valid 2 days. It covers the TX round trip, one round trip on the shuttle bus, and **unlimited cable car and ropeway rides**. Paying separately for the TX round trip, the bus round trip, and one cable car ride comes to about ¥5,500+, so it pays for itself even if you hike both ways

- Night 3 of 4

## Plan
All times are rough. The mountain has twin peaks, **Nantai-san** (871 m) and **Nyotai-san** (877 m, the higher one with the better view). They're joined by a short ridge with **Miyukigahara** and its teahouses in the middle. The plan is to hike up one trail and down another, with no cable car needed.

| Est. time | What |
|---|---|
| 7:30 AM | Leave the hotel (JR Ryogoku → Akihabara, 2 stops). Grab konbini breakfast and **onigiri/snacks for the trail** as a lunch backup |
| ~7:50 AM | At Akihabara, buy the **Mt. Tsukuba Ticket** from the TX machine, then take the **TX Rapid** to Tsukuba |
| ~8:35 AM | Arrive at Tsukuba. Last real toilet and konbini stop |
| 8:50 AM | **Shuttle bus** to Tsukubasan-jinja-iriguchi |
| ~9:30 AM | Walk up (~10 min) to **Tsukubasan Shrine**. The mountain itself is the object of worship, so the shrine sits at the base. Its maples and ginkgos peak mid-to-late Nov, so they'll be partly turned |
| ~9:50 AM | **Hike up the Miyukigahara Course**: ~2 km, ~610 m of gain, ~1.5 hrs. Steep and steady, mostly stone steps and roots through cedar forest, running alongside the cable car tracks. Maples on the middle slopes |
| ~11:20 AM | Reach **Miyukigahara**, the saddle between the peaks |
| 11:30 AM | Side trip to the **Nantai-san summit** (~15 min each way): a small shrine at the top and a **beech forest** that turns gold in early Nov. The Nature Research Trail loops around this peak if you want a longer detour |
| ~12:00 PM | **Lunch at the Miyukigahara teahouses** (most are 10:00–15:00). Get **Tsukuba udon**, the local udon with pork and Ibaraki vegetables. Weekday hours can be irregular, which is why you're carrying the konbini backup |
| ~12:45 PM | Walk the ridge to **Nyotai-san** (~20 min). The rocky summit has the **best view on the mountain**: the Kanto Plain, Lake Kasumigaura, and Tokyo/Skytree and Fuji on a clear day |
| ~1:20 PM | **Hike down the Shirakumobashi Course** back to the shrine (~1.5 hrs). The top section passes the famous **giant rocks**: **Benkei Nanamodori** (a boulder balanced overhead that Benkei supposedly hesitated under seven times), **Mother's Womb Rock**, and others. Then a long quiet forest descent |
| ~2:50 PM | Back at the shrine. Optional snack or souvenir stop on the shrine approach |
| ~3:15 PM | Bus from the **Shrine Entrance** back to Tsukuba Center. If you're slow, there are later buses, but the **last one is ~5:10 PM** |
| ~4:00 PM | TX Rapid back to Akihabara (~45 min) |
| ~5:00 PM | Back in Tokyo. Shower, then dinner near the hotel |

**Onsen option (instead of hiking down):** after Nyotai-san, walk back to Miyukigahara and take the **cable car** down (~8 min, covered by the ticket). That puts you at the shrine by ~1:45, in time for a day-use bath at the foot of the mountain. Both baths are near the shrine:
- **Tsukubasan Edoya**: 390+ year-old inn, ~5 min walk from the shrine. Entry 11:30, last reception **2:00 PM**, out by 3:00. ¥1,540, hand towel included
- **Tsukubasan Hotel Aokiya**: rooftop open-air bath with views out to Skytree and Fuji on a clear day. **12:00–3:00 PM**, ¥1,200

**Easier version:** take the cable car up and hike down Shirakumobashi. That keeps the rocks and the views and skips the steep climb.

**If running late:** drop the Nantai-san side trip, since Nyotai-san is the better summit. If it's past ~1:30 when you leave Nyotai, take the cable car or ropeway down instead of hiking.

**Weather call:** the view is the payoff. If Monday looks hazy or rainy and Tue Nov 10 looks clear, swap the two days.

## Foliage on Nov 9
Color moves down the mountain. Expect the **summit and upper slopes in color** (beech gold, some red maples), the **middle slopes and cable car line around peak**, and the **shrine area still a week or two early** (it peaks mid-to-late Nov). The **Momiji Festival's night cable car and maple light-up** (5–8 PM) runs **weekends and holidays only**, so you won't see it on a Monday.
