# Day 10 — Thu Nov 5 — Kaga Onsen day trip (Yamanaka, Yamashiro, Natadera), dinner with Jeff and Adam

Dan solo for the day. Jeff and Adam arrive from Kyoto/Osaka sometime today (time not confirmed) and meet up for dinner. Max flies home from Tokyo today.

## Plan
Local trains and buses only, no taxis. The order is **Yamanaka → Yamashiro → Natadera** because the buses force it: the only good bus from Yamashiro to Natadera is the 12:40 CAN Bus. The plan takes the **2:24 PM** bus out of Natadera; the **last one is 4:17 PM** (safety net). Times are from Navitime/Ekitan listings for a weekday in Oct 2026. Recheck the week before.

**More time in Yamashiro instead:** take the **10:50 AM** bus from Yamanaka (arrives 11:04) and have lunch in Yamashiro. That gives ~1h45 in Yamanaka and ~1.5 hrs in Yamashiro.

**If things slip:**
- **Late leaving Kanazawa:** take the 9:05 or 9:50 AM bus to Yamanaka and shorten the time there. The rest of the day doesn't change
- **Missed the 12:40 CAN Bus:** Natadera is out (there's no other way there and back in time). Stay longer in Yamashiro, then take the Hokutetsu bus back to the station (~13 min) and an earlier train
- **Rain:** Yamanaka's lacquerware shops and Iroha Soan are indoors, but the gorge walk and Natadera are outdoors. In heavy rain, consider staying in Kanazawa (Omicho, Nomura House, a gold-leaf workshop) and doing the Kaga trip another time

**Natadera in the morning instead (clunkier):** 7:32 AM train, then the 8:40 AM CAN Bus from Kaga Onsen Station → Natadera 9:09. Take the 10:57 bus back to the station (11:36), then wait until ~12:30 for a Hokutetsu bus to Yamanaka. The 3:50 PM bus from Yamanaka gets back to the station at 4:21 for the 4:23 or 4:53 PM train (Kanazawa 5:09 or 5:38). Yamashiro gets squeezed.

**For Jeff and Adam if they arrive early:** Nagamachi (the samurai lanes and Nomura House, open until 4:00 PM) is right by the restaurant. Higashi Chaya is ~10 min by bus from the station, but its shops close ~5–6 PM. Otherwise Nagamachi is on the [Nov 6](./2026-11-06.md) afternoon and Higashi Chaya on the [Nov 7](./2026-11-07.md) morning, both with Dan.

## Travel
- **Kanazawa ⇄ Kaga Onsen:** IR Ishikawa local, ~46 min, ¥880 each way, IC card OK. The Shinkansen (Tsurugi, ~18 min, ¥2,640 unreserved) saves ~30 min each way and costs ~¥3,500 more for the day. Kagayaki trains mostly skip Kaga Onsen
- **Hokutetsu Kaga Bus** (温泉山中 line): Kaga Onsen Station (stop 2) → Yamashiro Onsen (~13 min) → Yamanaka Onsen (~31 min), about twice an hour. **No IC cards**: cash or a contactless credit card
- **CAN Bus** (mountain route): only two rides needed today, ¥500 each, pay on board. A 1-day pass isn't worth it. Departures from Kaga Onsen Station toward Natadera: 8:40, 10:05, 12:25, 1:55, 3:25. Departures from Natadera to the station: 9:09, 10:57, 12:54, 2:24, **4:17 (last)**, per the official 2026 pamphlet. Unreserved, and buses can refuse riders when full in foliage season, so board one stop early at the Yamashiro South Exit stop (12:39) if you can
- Jeff and Adam from Kyoto/Osaka: Thunderbird to Tsuruga, then the Hokuriku Shinkansen to Kanazawa, ~2–2.5 hrs from Kyoto

**Cost for the day:** trains ¥1,760 (¥3,520 with the Tsurugi back) + Hokutetsu buses ~¥800 + CAN Bus ¥1,000 ≈ **¥3,560–5,320**, plus ~¥560 for Iroha Soan and ¥1,000 for Natadera. (Ekitan lists the IR fare as ¥1,330 vs Navitime's ¥880; IC card handles it either way.)
