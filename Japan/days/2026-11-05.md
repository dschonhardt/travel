# Day 10 — Thu Nov 5 — Kaga Onsen day trip (Natadera, Yamashiro, Yamanaka), dinner with Jeff and Adam

**Tentative.** Dan solo for the day. Jeff and Adam arrive from Kyoto/Osaka sometime today (time not confirmed) and meet up for dinner. Max flies home from Tokyo today.

## Plan
All times are rough estimates. The **CAN Bus** is the backbone of the day and doesn't run often, so build the times around its timetable.

| Est. time | What |
|---|---|
| 8:00 AM | Konbini or hotel breakfast |
| 8:30 AM | Hokuriku Shinkansen Kanazawa → **Kaga Onsen** (~20 min). Buy the **CAN Bus 1-day pass** (¥1,100) at the station |
| ~9:30 AM | **Natadera Temple** (mountain route): temple halls built into rock cliffs, caves, a pagoda and a garden known for its autumn color. Early Nov should be early-to-mid color. ~1–1.5 hrs |
| ~11:30 AM | **Yamashiro Onsen**: lunch near the bathhouse square, **Rosanjin's Iroha Soan** (small house museum of the potter-chef Rosanjin) and **Kutani pottery** shops. The public baths here are nude, so view the old bathhouse from outside |
| ~1:30 PM | **Yamanaka Onsen**: the **Kakusenkei Gorge** walk along the Daishoji River, from the Kurotani Bridge to the curved cypress **Korogi Bridge** (~1–1.5 hrs) |
| ~3:00 PM | Wander **Yuge Kaido**, the main street: **Yamanaka lacquerware** shops and snacks. No public bath (nude only, and the one private day-use bath, Ohanami Kyubei, is closed Thursdays) |
| ~4:30 PM | CAN Bus or the Kaga Onsen Bus back to Kaga Onsen Station, then the Shinkansen to Kanazawa |
| ~6:00 PM | Back in Kanazawa. Freshen up, meet **Jeff and Adam** |
| 7:00 PM | Dinner at **Ajidokoro Takasaki**, near Kazuemachi: the famous **crab cream croquette** (red snow crab, year-round) and crab from the tank. **Reservations required in crab season** |
| 8:30 PM | **Kazuemachi** after dark: the quiet geisha street along the Asano River, lantern-lit |

**Order is flexible:** go Yamanaka first and Natadera last if the CAN Bus times work better that way. If running late, cut Yamashiro to a quick lunch.

**For Jeff and Adam if they arrive early:** Higashi Chaya is a ~5 min walk from the restaurant, but its shops close ~5–6 PM. Otherwise it's on the [Nov 7](./2026-11-07.md) morning with Dan.

## Travel
- Kanazawa → Kaga Onsen: Hokuriku Shinkansen (~20 min). The cheaper IR Ishikawa local takes ~40 min
- **CAN Bus** from Kaga Onsen Station: mountain route (Natadera, Yamashiro, Yamanaka) and sea route. 1-day pass ¥1,100 covers all routes
- Backup to/from Yamanaka: Kaga Onsen Bus, ~30 min to the Yamanaka Onsen bus terminal. Kakusenkei is a short walk from there
- Jeff and Adam from Kyoto/Osaka: Thunderbird to Tsuruga, then the Hokuriku Shinkansen to Kanazawa, ~2–2.5 hrs from Kyoto
