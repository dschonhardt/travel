# Day 4 — Fri Oct 30 — Tokyo (Shinjuku Gyoen, Meiji Jingu, Harajuku, Shibuya, ramen festival, Shinjuku at night)

The Kamakura day trip moved to [Thu Oct 29](./2026-10-29.md), so today is free for the ramen festival, which opens today.

## Travel
- Hotel → **Sendagaya** (Shinjuku Gyoen): JR Chuo-Sobu local straight from Asakusabashi, ~20 min, no transfers
- Sendagaya → Meiji Jingu → Harajuku → Shibuya: all on foot
- Shibuya → **Komazawa-daigaku** (ramen festival): Tokyu Den-en-toshi Line, ~10 min, then ~15 min walk to the park
- Komazawa-daigaku → Shibuya → **Shinjuku**: Den-en-toshi Line, then JR Yamanote Line (~7 min)
- Shinjuku → hotel: JR Chuo-Sobu local straight to Asakusabashi, ~20 min

## Lodging
- **Hotel LiVEMAX Asakusabashi-Ekimae** (booked) — 1-1-14 Asakusabashi, Taito, Tokyo 111-0053
- Night 4 of 4. Check-out is Sat Oct 31 before 10:00 AM

## Plan
All times are rough estimates. The ramen festival is the one fixed point; everything else is flexible and can be dropped.

| Est. time | What |
|---|---|
| 8:15 AM | Konbini breakfast, then Chuo-Sobu local to Sendagaya |
| 9:00 AM | **Shinjuku Gyoen** at opening, via the Sendagaya Gate (¥500). Japanese garden, the big lawn, the greenhouse. Oct–Mar last entry is 4:00 PM, so morning is the time |
| 10:30 AM | Walk ~15 min to **Meiji Jingu**'s north (Kitasando) entrance. Go through the forest to the main hall, then out the south side at Harajuku |
| 11:45 AM | **Takeshita Street** |
| 12:30 PM | Walk down **Cat Street** to Shibuya |
| 1:15 PM | **Shibuya crossing** and Hachiko |
| 1:45 PM | Den-en-toshi Line to Komazawa-daigaku |
| 2:15 PM | Late lunch at the **Tokyo Ramen Festa**, Komazawa Olympic Park central plaza. Opening day. 11:00 AM–8:00 PM, free entry, ¥1,100 a bowl. This is its last year at Komazawa |
| 3:45 PM | Den-en-toshi Line back to Shibuya, JR to Shinjuku |
| 4:30 PM | **Metropolitan Government Building** South Observation Deck for sunset (~4:50 PM). Free, open until 10:00 PM, ~10 min walk from Shinjuku's west exit |
| 5:45 PM | **Kabukicho**: Godzilla head, Kabukicho Tower (17F deck and gelato) |
| 6:45 PM | Dinner in **Omoide Yokocho**: yakitori and beer |
| 8:15 PM | Drinks in **Golden Gai** |
| ~10:00 PM | Chuo-Sobu local back to Asakusabashi. Pack for Nagoya |

**Halloween weekend:** tonight is the night before Halloween. Expect extra crowds and possible street-drinking restrictions around Shibuya and Kabukicho. Shibuya is a midday stop and the Shinjuku drinking is inside bars, so it shouldn't change the plan.

**If running behind:** shorten Takeshita and Cat Street. The festival is open until 8:00 PM, so lunch can slide.

**For a lighter day:** skip the morning, go to the festival for lunch at 11:00 AM, and pick up the plan from Shibuya or Shinjuku afterward.
