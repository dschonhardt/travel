# Day 4 — Fri Oct 30 — Tokyo (Kamakura + Enoshima day trip, tentative)

## Plan
All times are rough estimates. Kamakura's leaves don't turn until early December, so nothing is lost by going now. Nikko is held for the Nov 9–11 block, when its foliage peaks.

| Est. time | What |
|---|---|
| 7:30 AM | Leave the hotel, Yokosuka Line from Bakurocho. Get off at **Kita-Kamakura**, one stop before Kamakura |
| 8:40 AM | **Engaku-ji**: large Zen temple complex right beside the station. Quiet in the morning |
| 9:30 AM | Walk ~5 min to **Jochi-ji**, a small moss-and-cedar Zen temple. The **Daibutsu Hiking Course** trailhead is right beside it |
| 9:45 AM | **Hike the Daibutsu course**: forested ridge trail with roots, some steep rocky sections, and a few glimpses toward the sea |
| ~10:15 AM | **Kuzuharaoka Shrine**, about halfway. Good rest stop |
| ~10:30 AM | **Genjiyama Park**. Optional ~10 min detour down to **Zeniarai Benten**, a cave shrine where people wash their money for luck, then rejoin the trail |
| ~11:15 AM | Trail comes out near Hase. Walk down to **Kotoku-in (Great Buddha)**: 13th-century bronze Buddha. Small extra fee to go inside it |
| 11:45 AM | **Hase-dera**: hillside temple with an ocean-view terrace and a small cave shrine |
| 12:45 PM | Lunch near Hase: **shirasu-don** (whitebait rice bowl), the local specialty. **Hase Shokudo**, right beside Hase Station, is the closest option. See Foods to try |
| 1:45 PM | Enoden from Hase to **Kamakura** (5 min) |
| 2:00 PM | **Komachi-dori** snack crawl (sausage, purple sweet potato soft-serve), then up to **Tsurugaoka Hachimangu**, Kamakura's main shrine (free). Kurumiko and Hato Sable shops are on the way. See Foods to try |
| 3:30 PM | Grab **Kamakura Wich** (the raisin sandwich) at the west exit, then Enoden from Kamakura to **Enoshima** (~25 min). Sit on the ocean side. It passes the Kamakurakokomae seaside railroad crossing |
| 4:10 PM | Walk the causeway onto **Enoshima**. **Tako senbei** at Asahi Honten on the way up (closes 6 PM). Sunset is ~4:50 PM. Enoshima Shrine, then the **Sea Candle** observation tower for the view back to the coast |
| 6:00 PM | Seafood dinner on Enoshima: more shirasu, or grilled shellfish |
| 7:15 PM | Head back: causeway, Shonan Monorail to Ofuna, Yokosuka Line to Bakurocho |
| ~8:30 PM | Back at the hotel. Pack for tomorrow's move to Nagoya |

**If running behind:** skip Engaku-ji and start straight at Jochi-ji. If you're short on time in the afternoon, drop Komachi-dori and Tsurugaoka and ride the Enoden from Hase straight to Enoshima (~20 min).

**Dropped for the hike:** Kencho-ji and the Hokokuji bamboo grove. Hokokuji is on the far east side of town and doesn't fit with the hike. Save it for a future trip.

## Travel
- Hotel → **Bakurocho Station** (~5–7 min walk) → JR Yokosuka Line (Sobu Rapid) straight to **Kita-Kamakura**, ~1 hr, no transfers, ~¥1,000 on Suica
- **Daibutsu Hiking Course** on foot: Kita-Kamakura → Great Buddha, ~3 km, ~1–1.5 hrs (confirmed open)
- Around Kamakura: Enoden train Hase → Kamakura → Enoshima
- Return: Shonan Monorail Shonan-Enoshima → Ofuna, then the Yokosuka Line straight back to Bakurocho, ~1h15 door to door

## Foods to try

In rough route order. Kamakura asks people not to eat while walking on crowded streets, so eat at or beside the stall.

| Food | What it is | Where on the route | Hours |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Shirasu-don** | Whitebait on rice, the Kamakura/Shonan specialty. Ask for **nama shirasu** (raw) if that morning's catch came in; the season runs through Dec | **Hase Shokudo**, right beside Enoden Hase Station (lunch). Enoshima restaurants have it too (dinner) | Check before going |
| **Sausage** | Grilled sausage on a stick | **Kamakura Ham Tomioka Shokai**, Komachi-dori (2-2-19 Komachi). Kamakura's ham maker since 1900; also sells croquettes to eat on the spot | 10:00 AM–6:00 PM, open daily |
| **Purple sweet potato soft-serve** (murasaki-imo) | Kamakura's signature soft-serve | **Imoyoshikan**, Komachi-dori (2-8-3 Komachi) | 10:00 AM–5:30 PM, open daily |
| **Kurumiko** | Walnut-caramel bars between butter cookies, in the squirrel packaging | **Kamakura Beniya Hachimangu-mae main store**, across from Tsurugaoka Hachimangu (1-12-4 Yukinoshita) | 9:30 AM–5:00 PM |
| **Hato Sable** | Dove-shaped butter cookies, Kamakura's classic souvenir since 1897. The main store sells pigeon goods you can't get elsewhere | **Toshimaya main store**, Wakamiya-oji near the second torii (2-11-19 Komachi), on the walk between Komachi-dori and the shrine | 9:00 AM–7:00 PM. Closed some Wednesdays, so Friday is fine |
| **Raisinwich** | Rum-soaked raisins and buttercream between sablé cookies. Now sold as **Kamakura Wich**: Kamakura Ogawaken renamed both the shop and the cookie on Nov 1, 2025 | **Kamakura Wich main store**, Onari-dori, ~1 min from Kamakura Station's west exit (8-1 Onari-machi). The Enoden leaves from the same side | 10:00 AM–6:00 PM |
| **Tako senbei** | Whole octopus pressed flat into a big cracker under a 1-ton press | **Asahi Honten**, Enoshima, on the Nakamise street just before the shrine's red torii (1-4-8 Enoshima). Expect a line | 9:00 AM–6:00 PM, closed Thursdays |

**Sources:** [Kamakura Ham Tomioka Shokai](https://www.kamakuraham-tomioka.co.jp/about/store/) · [Imoyoshikan](https://www.cocolocala.jp/spots/92279) · [Kamakura Beniya](https://beniya-ajisai.co.jp/shop/head/) · [Toshimaya](https://www.hato.co.jp/shop) · [Kamakura Wich rebrand](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000013.000166308.html) · [Kamakura Ogawaken stores](https://ogawaken.jp/category/store/) · [Asahi Honten](https://www.tabiulala.com/kamasho/spot077/) · [Hase Shokudo](https://haseshokudo.com/)
