**Beer spots to check out (Harajuku / Cat Street):**
- **Tokyo Beer Lab** (3-26-5 Jingumae): a small brewery in the Omotesando backstreets near the top of Cat Street. We've been before. Open daily 1:00–10:00 PM, so it fits after lunch, before or during the Cat Street walk ([Metropolis](https://metropolisjapan.com/best-craft-beer-in-tokyo/), [Time Out](https://www.timeout.jp/tokyo/ja/バー/tokyo-beer-lab))
- **Baird Beer Harajuku Taproom** (2F No Surrender Bldg, 1-20-13 Jingumae, ~3 min from Harajuku Station's Takeshita exit). **Weekdays it opens at 5:00 PM** (noon on weekends and holidays), so it doesn't fit this Friday's midday plan. Worth a trip back on another evening if one opens up ([Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/tokyo/bars-and-pubs/harajuku-taproom))

**Shibuya crossing view (free, ~15 min):** the glass-walled walkway between Shibuya Station and **Mark City** looks straight down on the crossing. Inside the station, follow the signs for Mark City or the Keio Inokashira Line. Taro Okamoto's 30 m *Myth of Tomorrow* mural (1969) is in the same passage. Walk the crossing at street level first, then go up and watch the next wave. The paid rooftop options are **Shibuya Sky** and the **MAGNET by SHIBUYA109** rooftop: higher up, but they cost money and take longer ([Islands.com](https://www.islands.com/1834565/best-way-how-see-tokyo-japan-shibuya-crossing-above-without-spending-yen-free))

**Halloween weekend:** tonight is the night before Halloween. Expect extra crowds and possible street-drinking restrictions around Shibuya and Kabukicho. Shibuya is a midday stop and the Shinjuku drinking is inside bars, so it shouldn't change the plan.

**If running behind:** shorten Cat Street or the Shibuya stop. Aim to reach Shinjuku by ~4:30 PM for sunset.
