# Day 15 — Tue Nov 10 — Tokyo (Chuo Line: Nakano, Nogata, Koenji, Kichijoji)

**Draft (Oct 24, replaces the Oct 23 draft).** A slow day after the Tsukuba hike, all on the JR Chuo Line. Late start at **Nakano Broadway** for retro games, **Nogata Hope** ramen for lunch (Adam's pick), an afternoon of Koenji thrift shops, then a soak at **Kosugi-yu** to fix the hiking legs. Evening in Kichijoji's **Harmonica Yokocho** (the Omoide Yokocho match), and **Akiyoshi** yakitori in Nakano (Adam's pick) on the way home. The Chuo-Sobu local runs straight from Ryogoku to every stop, so there are no transfers. Hours are from Google Maps unless marked.

## Plan
All times are rough estimates.

| Est. time | What |
|---|---|
| 10:30 AM | Slow start. Konbini or hotel breakfast |
| 11:15 AM | JR Chuo-Sobu local from Ryogoku to **Nakano** (~35 min, no transfer) |
| 12:00 PM | Walk up the **Nakano Sun Mall** arcade to **Nakano Broadway** (10 AM–8 PM). **Mandarake** opens at 12:00 (12–8 PM, daily): manga, figures, retro games and trading cards across ~25–35 small shops on floors 2–4. ~1.5 hrs |
| 1:30 PM | Walk ~20–25 min north-west to **Nogata Hope** main shop, 2-58-4 Nogata (walk time not checked; a taxi is ~5 min) |
| 2:00 PM | **Lunch: Nogata Hope** 🟢 (11 AM–3 AM daily). Triple-soup tonkotsu, ~¥740. **Cash or PayPay only** |
| 2:45 PM | Walk ~15 min south into **Koenji**'s north side: the **Kita-Kore Building** (11 AM–6 PM, wild secondhand fashion), the vintage and thrift shops on the north-side streets, and **Koenji Hikawa Shrine** (8 AM–5 PM; Japan's only shrine to the weather). Then under the tracks to the south side's **Pal** and **Look** arcades |
| 4:30 PM | **Kosugi-yu** sento (Tue 2 PM–1:30 AM, closed Thursdays). Tattoo-friendly neighborhood bath. Hot water for the hiking legs, ~1 hr. Towels can be rented; bring a small one to be safe |
| 5:45 PM | Chuo Line Koenji → **Kichijoji** (~10 min) |
| 6:00 PM | **Harmonica Yokocho**: a tight grid of tiny bars and stalls by the north exit. Bar-hop for a couple of hours. Snacks along the way: **Minmin** gyoza (Tue 11:30 AM–3 PM and 4–8:30 PM, closed Thursdays) and a menchi-katsu from **Satou** (10 AM–7:30 PM; get there before it closes). Each bar keeps its own hours, and Google's 10 AM–8 PM for the alley as a whole is only rough. Nightcap option: **YONA YONA BEER WORKS Kichijoji** (Yo-Ho Brewing taproom, 11:30 AM–10 PM daily) |
| 8:00 PM | Chuo-Sobu local back east to **Nakano** (~10 min) |
| 8:15 PM | **Dinner: Akiyoshi Nakano** 🟢 (5-54-3 Nakano, Fujita Bldg 1F, 03-3385-0071; Tue 5–10:30 PM). Fukui-style yakitori, ~5 min from the station. Last order not checked |
| ~10:15 PM | Chuo-Sobu local straight back to Ryogoku (~35 min) |

**Daylight version:** skip Kosugi-yu and get to Kichijoji by ~4:00 PM for **Inokashira Park** (the pond and early autumn color) before sunset at ~4:40, then Harmonica Yokocho at dusk. **Iseya Koen Store** by the park does old-school yakitori (Tue 12–9 PM; the main Iseya Sohonten is **closed Tuesdays**).

**Lighter dinner swap:** if the Harmonica snacks fill everyone up, eat at Akiyoshi first at 5:30 PM instead (open from 5:00) and finish in Kichijoji. That means riding Koenji → Nakano → Kichijoji → home, which is still all one line.

**Left out:** Kichijoji's **Beer Boy** (Google Maps has no listing for it, so it may have closed; the Shibuya branch is on [Oct 30](./2026-10-30.md)) and the **Ghibli Museum** (closed Nov 4–18 and every Tuesday). Shimokitazawa, Udon Shin and Kanoya would suit Jeff and Adam on Mon Nov 9 while Dan is on Mt. Tsukuba.

## Travel
- Ryogoku → Nakano: JR Chuo-Sobu local, direct (~35 min)
- Nakano → Nogata Hope → Koenji: on foot (~20–25 min, then ~15 min; not checked)
- Koenji → Kichijoji → Nakano: Chuo Line, ~10 min each way
- Nakano → Ryogoku: JR Chuo-Sobu local, direct (~35 min). Last trains are around midnight
