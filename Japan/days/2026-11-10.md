# Day 15 — Tue Nov 10 — Tokyo (Chuo Line: Nakano, Koenji, Kichijoji)

**Draft (Oct 24, replaces the Oct 23 draft; morning added Oct 25).** A slow day after the Tsukuba hike, all on the JR Chuo Line. The morning has stops on the way out: **Koishikawa Korakuen** garden and **Kagurazaka** at Iidabashi, then an hour of carp fishing in the old castle moat at the **Ichigaya Fish Center**. Then **Nogata Hope** ramen for lunch in Nakano (Adam's pick), **Nakano Broadway** for retro games, an afternoon of Koenji thrift shops, then **Inokashira Park** in Kichijoji before sunset. Evening in Kichijoji's **Harmonica Yokocho** (the Omoide Yokocho match), and **Akiyoshi** yakitori in Nakano (Adam's pick) on the way home. The Chuo-Sobu local runs straight from Ryogoku to every stop, so there are no transfers. Hours are from Google Maps unless marked.

## Plan
All times are rough estimates.

| Est. time | What |
|---|---|
| 8:45 AM | Konbini or hotel breakfast |
| 9:30 AM | JR Chuo-Sobu local from Ryogoku to **Iidabashi** (~15 min, no transfer) |
| 9:45 AM | **Koishikawa Korakuen** (Tue 9 AM–4:30 PM), ~8 min walk from Iidabashi. One of Tokyo's oldest stroll gardens (begun in the 1600s): the red Engetsukyo moon bridge, ponds and the first autumn color. ~45 min |
| 10:30 AM | Walk back past the station to **Kagurazaka**, the old geisha district: the cobbled back alleys off the main slope, and **Akagi Shrine** (Tue 9 AM–5 PM), Kengo Kuma's modern glass shrine near the top. Snack option: **Gojuban**'s giant pork buns or **Kinozen**'s matcha bavarois (Google gave no clear hours for either, so check on the day) |
| ~10:50 AM | Walk ~15 min to **Ichigaya** along the old outer moat (or take the Chuo-Sobu local one stop) |
| 11:00 AM | **Ichigaya Fish Center** 🎣 (weekdays 11 AM–7 PM, last entry 6 PM, may close early in rain; [official site](https://www.ichigaya-fc.com/hp2025/carp.html), Oct 25). Carp fishing in the castle moat right beside the tracks. **¥1,200 per adult per hour**, rod and bait included; extra bait ¥100; +30 min ¥600. Catch and release, no reservation, weekdays are quiet. Ichigaya Station exit 4. ~1 hr |
| 12:10 PM | JR Chuo-Sobu local Ichigaya → **Nakano** (~15 min, no transfer) |
| 12:30 PM | **Lunch: Nogata Hope Nakano branch** 🟢 (野方ホープ 中野店, 2-2-1 Arai; Tue 11 AM–1:30 AM, Google Maps). Walk up the **Nakano Sun Mall** arcade and through Broadway; it's ~2–3 min past Broadway's north end. Classic shoyu tonkotsu **元 (Gen) ¥950**; richer **濃 (Koku)** with seafood broth ¥980; **焦がし味噌** charred-miso tonkotsu (fall–winter limited) ¥1,080; everything-on **旨盛** ¥1,380. Tax included, [official menu](https://www.nogata-hope.com/menu), Oct 25. **Cash or PayPay only**. The original main shop (2-58-4 Nogata) is a ~25 min walk or ~5 min taxi away. Only worth it if Adam wants the original |
| 1:15 PM | Back to **Nakano Broadway** (10 AM–8 PM). **Mandarake** opens at 12:00 (12–8 PM, daily): manga, figures, retro games and trading cards across ~25–35 small shops on floors 2–4. ~1.25 hrs |
| 2:30 PM | JR Chuo Line Nakano → **Koenji** (1 stop, ~2 min), north exit. Then **Koenji**'s north side: the **Kita-Kore Building** (11 AM–6 PM, wild secondhand fashion), the vintage and thrift shops on the north-side streets, and **Koenji Hikawa Shrine** (8 AM–5 PM; Japan's only shrine to the weather). Then under the tracks to the south side's **Pal** and **Look** arcades |
| 3:45 PM | Chuo Line Koenji → **Kichijoji** (~10 min) |
| 4:00 PM | **Inokashira Park** (south exit, ~5 min walk): the pond and early autumn color before sunset at ~4:40. Optional yakitori stop at **Iseya Koen Store** by the park (Tue 12–9 PM; the main Iseya Sohonten is **closed Tuesdays**) |
| ~5:00 PM | **Harmonica Yokocho** at dusk: a tight grid of tiny bars and stalls by the north exit. Bar-hop until ~6:45. Snacks along the way: **Minmin** gyoza (Tue 11:30 AM–3 PM and 4–8:30 PM, closed Thursdays) and a menchi-katsu from **Satou** (10 AM–7:30 PM, so go early). Each bar keeps its own hours, and Google's 10 AM–8 PM for the alley as a whole is only rough. Craft beer option: **YONA YONA BEER WORKS Kichijoji** (Yo-Ho Brewing taproom, 11:30 AM–10 PM daily) |
| 6:45 PM | Chuo-Sobu local back east to **Nakano** (~10 min) |
| 7:00 PM | **Dinner: Akiyoshi Nakano** 🟢 (5-54-3 Nakano, Fujita Bldg 1F, 03-3385-0071; Tue 5–10:30 PM). Fukui-style yakitori, ~5 min from the station. Last order not checked |
| ~9:00 PM | Optional nightcap at **Nakano Beer Kobo**, a small brewpub near Nakano Station (Tue 5–11 PM, closed Mondays) |
| ~9:30–10:00 PM | Chuo-Sobu local straight back to Ryogoku (~35 min) |

**Left out:** the **Kosugi-yu** sento in Koenji (skipped Oct 25), Kichijoji's **Beer Boy** (Google Maps has no listing for it, so it may have closed; the Shibuya branch is on [Oct 30](./2026-10-30.md)) and the **Ghibli Museum** (closed Nov 4–18 and every Tuesday). Other morning options looked at on Oct 25: the **Edo-Tokyo Museum** or the **Sumida Hokusai Museum** by the hotel (both Tue 9:30 AM–5:30 PM, closed Mondays), or **Ochanomizu**'s **Yushima Seido** (Tue 9:30 AM–5 PM) and **Kanda Myojin** (Tue 9 AM–4 PM) as a backup for the Iidabashi stop. Shimokitazawa, Udon Shin and Kanoya would suit Jeff and Adam on Mon Nov 9 while Dan is on Mt. Tsukuba.

## Travel
- Ryogoku → Iidabashi: JR Chuo-Sobu local, direct (~15 min). Iidabashi → Ichigaya: ~15 min walk along the moat, or 1 stop. Ichigaya → Nakano: Chuo-Sobu local, direct (~15 min)
- Nakano Station → Nogata Hope Nakano branch: up the Sun Mall and through Broadway, ~2–3 min past its north end. Then Nakano → Koenji: JR Chuo Line, 1 stop (~2 min)
- Koenji → Kichijoji → Nakano: Chuo Line, ~10 min each way
- Nakano → Ryogoku: JR Chuo-Sobu local, direct (~35 min). Last trains are around midnight
