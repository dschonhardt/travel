# Day 15 — Tue Nov 10 — Tokyo (Chuo Line: Kichijoji, Koenji, Nakano, Ogikubo)

**Draft (Oct 23).** A slower day after the Tsukuba hike. Chains the Chuo Line neighborhoods (Kichijoji, Koenji, Nakano Broadway) with Adam's **Nogata Hope** ramen and **Akiyoshi** yakitori. The JR Chuo-Sobu local from Ryogoku runs straight out to Nakano, so getting there is easy.

## Plan
All times are rough estimates.

| Est. time | What |
|---|---|
| 9:30 AM | JR Chuo-Sobu local / Chuo Rapid from Ryogoku to **Kichijoji** (~45 min) |
| 10:15 AM | **Inokashira Park** (rowboats, autumn color around mid-Nov), then **Harmonica Yokocho** in daylight and a menchi-katsu from **Satou** |
| 12:30 PM | Chuo Line to **Koenji** (~6 min), walk ~15 min north to **Nogata Hope** for ramen (cash only) |
| 1:45 PM | Walk back through Koenji's north-side shopping streets: vintage, thrift, sukajan jackets |
| 3:30 PM | One stop to **Nakano Broadway**: Mandarake (12:00–20:00) and the retro game and figure shops |
| 5:30 PM | **Akiyoshi Nakano** (5-54-3 Nakano, 03-3385-0071) yakitori dinner, open daily from 17:00. The Ogikubo branch is no longer on the chain's list |
| 7:30 PM | Optional: back to Koenji for **Kosugi-yu** sento and a bar, or ride the Chuo-Sobu local back to Ryogoku (~35 min, no transfer) |

**Alternative dinner close to the hotel:** **Yamariki** nikomi in Morishita (17:00–22:30, cash only, queue ~1 hr before opening; phone 03-3633-1638 about booking). Akiyoshi Shinkoiwa is closed Tuesdays.

**Sunday swap to consider:** Koenji's **Ashiba Matsuri** (Okinawan festival, Orion beer, Eisa drums) is **Sun Nov 8, 11:00–18:30**. Swapping Koenji onto Sunday catches it. See [tokyo-neighborhoods.md](../tokyo-neighborhoods.md).

**Morning alternative:** Tuesday is the best day for **Tsukiji Outer Market** (closed Sundays). Breakfast there at ~8:00, then head to Kichijoji at ~10:00.

## Travel
- Ryogoku → Kichijoji: JR Chuo-Sobu local, or change to the Chuo Rapid at Ochanomizu (~45 min)
- Kichijoji → Koenji → Nakano: all on the Chuo Line, a few minutes each
- Nakano → Ryogoku: JR Chuo-Sobu local, direct (~35 min)
