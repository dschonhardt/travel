# Day 16 — Wed Nov 11 — Tokyo (departure)

**Draft (Oct 23, updated Oct 24).** Stay near the hotel and the airport line: coffee in Kiyosumi-Shirakawa or a wander in Kuramae, a last bowl at **Yuzu Lab Noodles** (bookends the trip, since it was the first full-day dinner on Oct 28), then the Asakusa Line to Haneda.

## Plan
All times are rough estimates.

| Est. time | What |
|---|---|

| **5:15 PM** | **Delta 120** HND → MSP |

**Kuramae instead of Kiyosumi:** Dandelion Chocolate and the Kakimori notebook shop, 1 stop on the Oedo Line. Kuramae Station is also on the Asakusa Line, but the bags are back at the hotel either way.
