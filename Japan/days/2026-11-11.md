# Day 16 — Wed Nov 11 — Tokyo (departure)

**Draft (Oct 23).** Stay near the hotel and the airport line: coffee in Kiyosumi-Shirakawa or a wander in Kuramae, then the Asakusa Line to Haneda.

## Plan
All times are rough estimates.

| Est. time | What |
|---|---|
| 9:30 AM | Pack and check out (before 10:00). Leave bags at the APA front desk |
| 10:00 AM | Oedo Line 2 stops to **Kiyosumi-Shirakawa**: **Blue Bottle** or **Allpress**, then **Kiyosumi Garden** |
| 11:30 AM | Optional: one Hanzomon Line stop to **Ningyocho**, for taiyaki on Amazake Yokocho |
| 12:30 PM | Last meal: **Ryogoku Edo Noren** by the station (chanko, sushi, monja), which keeps the bag pickup close |
| 1:45 PM | Grab bags. JR Sobu Line one stop to Asakusabashi, then the Toei Asakusa Line through onto Keikyu to **HND Terminal 3** (~45–50 min) |
| ~2:45 PM | At Haneda, ~2.5 hrs before the flight |
| **5:15 PM** | **Delta 120** HND → MSP |

**Kuramae instead of Kiyosumi:** Dandelion Chocolate and the Kakimori notebook shop, 1 stop on the Oedo Line. Kuramae Station is also on the Asakusa Line, but the bags are back at the hotel either way.

## Travel
- Delta 120: departs HND 5:15 PM → arrives MSP 1:23 PM the same day (booked)
- Route to HND: JR Sobu Line Ryogoku → Asakusabashi (1 stop), then the Toei Asakusa Line, which runs through onto Keikyu straight to Haneda (~45–50 min total, roughly ¥600). Alternatively, walk ~13 min to Kuramae and board the Asakusa Line there with no JR leg. Exact route not confirmed yet. Not every Asakusa Line train goes through to Haneda, so check the destination board

## Lodging
- Check out: **APA Hotel & Resort Ryogoku Eki Tower**, before 10:00 AM. Ask the front desk to hold bags until you leave for the airport
- Most of the day is free before the flight
