## Travel
- Check out: Nagoya hotel before 10:00 AM (aim for ~8:15)
- Hotel → Nagoya Station: Sakuradori subway from **Hisaya-odori**, 3 stops (~6 min), then ~10 min to the JR gates. Allow ~10 min at the ticket machine to print the Hida 5 ticket; be on the platform by 9:25
- **Booked: Hida 5**, departs Nagoya **9:39 AM** → arrives Takayama ~12:15 PM (~2h35). Reserved seat, ¥6,140–6,340 depending on JR's season tier (~$41–42)
- The train reverses at Gifu (~20 min in). The river/gorge views are on the right side heading toward Takayama (A seats)
