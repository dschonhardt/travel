# Day 7 — Mon Nov 2 — Nagoya → Takayama (Hida Express, old town, sake breweries)

**Group split:** Dan goes to Takayama. Jeff, Adam, and Max go to Kyoto/Osaka.

## Travel
- Check out: Nagoya hotel before 10:00 AM
- Hida Limited Express Nagoya → Takayama, ~2.5 hrs. ¥5,600–6,150 reserved (~$37–41 pp)
- Suggested departure: **Hida 3, ~9:43 AM** from Nagoya → arrives Takayama ~12:43 PM (time for a quick breakfast near Nagoya Station before boarding)
- Reserve seat — foliage season, fills up

## Lodging
- Check in: **Hida Takayama Washington Hotel Plaza** (booked) — 5-20 Hanasato-cho, Takayama 506-0026 (across from JR Takayama Station). Check-in 3:00 PM; drop bags early if needed
- Night 1 of 2

## Plan
All times are rough estimates.

| Est. time | What |
|---|---|
| 8:30 AM | Grab breakfast near Nagoya Station before boarding |
| 9:43 AM | **Hida Limited Express** departs Nagoya — scenic mountain gorge ride, treat it as part of the sightseeing |
| 12:43 PM | Arrive Takayama. Drop bags at hotel or coin locker at the station |
| 1:00 PM | **Lunch** near old town |
| 2:00 PM | **Sanmachi Suji old town** — preserved Edo-era merchant streets; sake breweries marked by cedar balls (sugidama) hanging out front. 45–60 min wander |
| 3:00 PM | **Sake brewery tastings** along Sanmachi Suji — breweries close ~5 PM and some stop tastings ~4 PM, so start with the ones you care most about (see Sake Breweries in [city-ideas.md](../city-ideas.md)) |
| 4:30 PM | **Hida beef skewer** from a street vendor in old town — one of the best cheap bites in Japan. Grab it before ~5 PM, when the old-town stalls close |
| 5:30 PM | Check in to hotel (if not already done), freshen up |
| 7:00 PM | **Dinner** — old town izakaya or kaiseki. Splurge option: **Ryotei Susaki** (Michelin ★★, ~¥13,000 / ~$87 for the 11-dish Honzen Kuzushi course) — tatami seating, kimono service, shoes off; book well ahead (reservation only, dinner 5–9 PM, last entry 7 PM). **Backups if Susaki is full:** ① **Shiki no Gochisou Mitsuiwa** — kaiseki with Hida beef fillet/loin steak + seasonal seafood, ~¥6,000 basic dinner / ~¥10,000–20,000 full kaiseki, ~4 min walk from the station, dinner 5:00–9:30 PM, closes irregularly, reserve ahead ② **Hida Takayama Sakana** — seasonal Hida kaiseki (autumn mushrooms + A5 Hida beef), ~¥15,000–25,000, reservation only, 1126-1 Echigo-machi ③ **Kyoya** — no-reservation fallback; rustic old house, Hida beef grilled at the table + hoba miso, open Mondays (closed Tue). For any of these, check they'll book a party of 1 |
| 8:30 PM | **Night walk: Sanmachi Suji after dark** — old town is lantern-lit and nearly empty once the shops close. Walk the three parallel lanes (Ichino-machi, Nino-machi, Sanno-machi) north → south. Best photo walk of the day. ~30 min |
| 9:00 PM | **Nakabashi Bridge** — the red bridge at the south end of old town, softly lit at night. Linger on the Miyagawa riverside, then cross to the west bank. ~15 min |
| 9:15 PM | **Nightcap at Red Hill Pub** — 2-4 Sowa-machi, ~5–10 min walk west of the bridge. Small, friendly bar, local sake, jazz/rock/old pop, easy to meet other travelers. 7 PM–midnight. *Alt:* wander the tiny izakaya lanes in Sowa-machi 2-chome / Asahi-machi, or Dekonaru Yokocho (food-stall alley near the station, some stalls till ~11) |
| 10:00 PM | Walk back to hotel (~10 min). **Early night** — Kamikochi buses tomorrow can't be reserved, so you want an early start |
