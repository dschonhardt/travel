# Day 11 — Fri Nov 6 — Kanazawa (Kenrokuen, castle, museums, Ninja Temple, Katamachi)

**Tentative.** The one full day with Jeff and Adam in Kanazawa. Crab season opens at midnight tonight, but local crab isn't in shops until tomorrow (see [city-ideas.md](../city-ideas.md)).

## Plan
All times are rough estimates.

| Est. time | What |
|---|---|
| 7:45 AM | Bus from the station (or ~25 min walk) to Kenrokuen |
| 8:00 AM | **Kenrokuen Garden** at opening (8:00 AM from mid-Oct): one of Japan's three great gardens, best before the tour groups. **Yukitsuri** ropes going up on the pines, with crews working. ~1.5 hrs |
| 9:45 AM | **Kanazawa Castle Park** across the bridge: free to walk the grounds, Ishikawa Gate, the rebuilt turrets |
| 10:45 AM | **Ishiura Shrine**: the tunnel of red torii, right by the museum |
| 11:00 AM | **21st Century Museum of Contemporary Art**: the free zone and the **Swimming Pool** (the view from below needs a timed ticket; get one when you arrive). Open late on Fridays |
| 12:30 PM | Walk or bus to **Omicho Market**. Lunch: **kaisendon** (nodoguro, amaebi). Pre-season, so local crab isn't in yet |
| 2:00 PM | Over the Sai River to **Myoryuji "Ninja Temple"** for a guided tour (~40 min). **Book ahead** |
| 3:00 PM | Wander **Nishi Chaya** and the temple streets of **Teramachi** |
| 4:00 PM | Option: **Fukumitsuya Sake Brewery** tour and tasting (Kanazawa's oldest, since 1625; ~1 hr, reservation required). Or rest at the hotel |
| 6:00 PM | **Korinbo Tree Fantasy** lights on the walk into Katamachi |
| 6:30 PM | Dinner in **Katamachi**. Options: **Sake & Shokuyujin Minato** (whole boiled crab, reserve a day ahead), a **Kanazawa oden** place, or **jibu-ni** (duck stew). Ichinokura is the walk-in fallback |
| 8:30 PM | Drinks in Katamachi, Kanazawa's main nightlife area |

**If running behind:** drop the 21st Century Museum or Teramachi. Kenrokuen early is the priority.

**Quieter add-on near Kenrokuen:** the **D.T. Suzuki Museum** (~45 min, Zen museum with a "water mirror" courtyard).

## Travel
- All on foot or by city bus. The **Kanazawa Loop Bus** and the **Kenrokuen Shuttle** leave from the station's east exit. A one-day bus pass is worth it for 3+ rides
