# Day 1 — Tue Oct 27 — Tokyo (arrival)

**Visit Japan Web:** [log in here](https://services.digital.go.jp/en/visit-japan-web/) (official Digital Agency page, then hit the login button). Have the immigration and customs QR codes up before landing. The site has no app, so ignore any "Visit Japan Web" app.

## Plan
All times are rough estimates. Adjust once the airport → hotel route is confirmed.

| Est. time | What |
|---|---|
| 2:35 PM | Land at HND (booked) |
| 2:35–3:45 PM | Immigration, customs, bags. Turn on the eSIM (already bought) for data, and pick up a transit IC card if needed |

Yuzu Lab Noodles is skipped tonight (it's dinner Oct 28 and the last meal Nov 11). Backup dinner: **Sushi Uogashi Nihon-ichi, Atre Akihabara** (standing sushi at Akihabara Station, Tue 10 AM–10:30 PM, Google Maps), right at the arcades.
