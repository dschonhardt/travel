# Day 5 — Sat Oct 31 — Tokyo → Nagoya (Shinkansen, Osu, misokatsu, Sakae, tebasaki)

## Plan
All times are rough estimates. Today covers Osu and Sakae, which leaves Sunday for Nagoya Castle and the bigger outings (museums, sake tour, or Nabana no Sato).

| Est. time | What |
|---|---|
| 7:45 AM | **Gyudon breakfast** near the hotel (Matsuya is open 24 hrs), last one in Tokyo for now |
| 8:30 AM | Check out, train to Tokyo Station |
| 9:00 AM | Tokyo Station: grab snacks or an ekiben for the ride |
| ~9:30 AM | **Nozomi** to Nagoya. Sit on the right side (seats D/E) for **Mt. Fuji**, about 40–45 min in |
| ~11:10 AM | Arrive Nagoya. Sakuradori Line to Marunouchi, drop bags at the hotel |
| ~11:40 AM | Subway to **Yaba-cho** (Sakuradori Line to Hisaya-odori, then Meijo Line 2 stops), ~15 min |
| 12:00 PM | Lunch at **Yabaton Yaba-cho Honten** (3-6-18 Osu, ~5 min from Yaba-cho Station): misokatsu at the original shop, plus the curry bun. Open daily 11:00 AM–9:00 PM. Saturday noon is busy, so expect a line |
| 1:15 PM | Walk west into Osu (~10 min): **Osu Shopping Arcade**, then **Osu Kannon** temple. **Osu Uiro main store** (2-18-42 Osu, 10:00 AM–6:00 PM, open daily) for **uiro**, the steamed rice cake: grab the 5-flavor bite-size box (¥550). Take your time; there's slack until the tower |
| 3:15 PM | Walk north through **Hisaya-odori Park** to Sakae (~25 min from Osu Kannon) |
| 3:45 PM | **Oasis 21** "Water Spaceship" rooftop (free, 10:00 AM–9:00 PM) |
| 4:30 PM | **Mirai Tower** observation decks for sunset (~5:00 PM) and the lights coming on. ¥1,300, Saturdays open until 9:40 PM |
| 5:45 PM | Sakuradori Line one stop back to the hotel. Check in, **hot spring bath** |
| 7:30 PM | Dinner: **tebasaki** at **Furaibo Sakae** (4-5-8 Sakae, ~3 min from Sakae Station exit 12). Fri/Sat 5:00 PM–midnight. Reserve for 4 |
| 9:00 PM | Optional: a beer in Sakae, or an early night |

**Halloween:** it's Halloween night. Sakae and Osu get costumed crowds, but it's far calmer than Shibuya. Nothing to plan around.

**If the train runs late:** skip Oasis 21 and go straight up the tower.

## Foods to try

| Food | What it is | Where today |
|---|---|---|
| **Misokatsu** (Hatcho miso) | Pork cutlet under a thick red miso sauce | **Yabaton Yaba-cho Honten**, noon lunch |
| **Uiro** | Chewy steamed rice-flour cake, Nagoya's classic sweet | **Osu Uiro main store** in the Osu arcade |
| **Tebasaki** | Crispy wings with a sweet soy glaze, pepper and sesame | **Furaibo Sakae**, dinner. Eat them by pulling the two bones apart |
| **Doteni** (Hatcho miso) | Beef tendon or offal stewed in red miso | On the menu at most Nagoya izakaya, often Furaibo too. Order it if you see it |

More red miso and the Chita beef dinner are on [Nov 1](./2026-11-01.md).

## Travel
- Hotel → **Tokyo Station**: JR Chuo-Sobu local one stop to Akihabara, then Yamanote or Keihin-Tohoku two stops. ~15 min
- **Tokaido Shinkansen** Tokyo → Nagoya, Nozomi, ~1h40. ¥10,560 unreserved / ¥11,300 reserved (~$70–75 pp one-way). Aim for a ~9:30 AM departure, arriving ~11:10 AM, so lunch at Yabaton lands around noon (exact train not picked yet)
- Nagoya Station → hotel: Sakuradori subway line two stops to **Marunouchi**
- Around Nagoya: Tsurumai Line Marunouchi → Osu Kannon (2 stops); Osu → Sakae on foot; Sakuradori Line Hisaya-odori → Marunouchi (1 stop) back to the hotel
