## Travel
- Hotel → **Tokyo Station**: JR Chuo-Sobu local one stop to Akihabara, then Yamanote or Keihin-Tohoku two stops. ~15 min
- **Tokaido Shinkansen** Tokyo → Nagoya, Nozomi, ~1h40. ¥10,560 unreserved / ¥11,300 reserved (~$70–75 pp one-way). Aim for a ~10:30 AM departure, arriving ~12:10 PM (exact train not picked yet)
- Nagoya Station → hotel: Sakuradori subway line two stops to **Marunouchi**
- Around Nagoya: Tsurumai Line Marunouchi → Osu Kannon (2 stops); Osu → Sakae on foot; Sakuradori Line Hisaya-odori → Marunouchi (1 stop) back to the hotel

## Lodging
- Check out: Tokyo hotel before 10:00 AM
- Check in: **Hotel LiVEMAX PREMIUM Nagoya Marunouchi** (booked) — 3 Chome-16-22 Marunouchi, Naka Ward, Nagoya, Aichi 460-0002
- Night 1 of 2. Check-in after 3:00 PM (drop bags on arrival)

## Plan
All times are rough estimates. Today covers Osu and Sakae, which leaves Sunday for Nagoya Castle and the bigger outings (museums, sake tour, or Nabana no Sato).

| Est. time | What |
|---|---|
| 8:30 AM | **Gyudon breakfast** near the hotel (Matsuya or Yoshinoya), last one in Tokyo for now |
| 9:30 AM | Check out, train to Tokyo Station |
| 10:00 AM | Tokyo Station: grab snacks or an ekiben for the ride |
| ~10:30 AM | **Nozomi** to Nagoya. Sit on the right side (seats D/E) for **Mt. Fuji**, about 40–45 min in |
| ~12:10 PM | Arrive Nagoya. Subway to Marunouchi, drop bags at the hotel |
| 1:15 PM | Tsurumai Line to **Osu Kannon**: the temple, then east through the **Osu Shopping Arcade**. **Osu Uiro** for the steamed rice cake on the way |
| 2:15 PM | Late lunch at **Yabaton Yaba-cho Honten** (3-6-18 Osu): misokatsu at the original shop, plus the curry bun. Open daily 11:00 AM–9:00 PM. A late lunch should dodge the worst of the Saturday line |
| 3:30 PM | Walk north through **Hisaya-odori Park** to Sakae (~15 min) |
| 4:00 PM | **Oasis 21** "Water Spaceship" rooftop (free, 10:00 AM–9:00 PM) |
| 4:30 PM | **Mirai Tower** observation decks for sunset (~5:00 PM) and the lights coming on. ¥1,300, Saturdays open until 9:40 PM |
| 5:45 PM | Sakuradori Line one stop back to the hotel. Check in, **hot spring bath** |
| 7:30 PM | Dinner: **tebasaki** at **Furaibo Sakae** (4-5-8 Sakae, ~3 min from Sakae Station exit 12). Fri/Sat 5:00 PM–midnight. Reserve for 4 |
| 9:00 PM | Optional: a beer in Sakae, or an early night |

**Halloween:** it's Halloween night. Sakae and Osu get costumed crowds, but it's far calmer than Shibuya. Nothing to plan around.

**If the train runs late:** skip Oasis 21 and go straight up the tower.
